## Flag Act of 2091
Due to the increase in the number of states, and therefore the increase in the number of stars, the US Congress held debates over changes to the US Flag Code. A number of proposed changes were made, the most radical being the Great Field Flag, but ultimately the decision was reached to maintain the existing field's dimensions and scale the stars down by 20%.
