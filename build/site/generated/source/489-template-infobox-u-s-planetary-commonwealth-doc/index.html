# Parameters
The parameters below are required unless marked as ''optional''.

## Name
*'''Name''': the common name of the state
*'''Fullname''': the full name of the state (such as "State of California" or "Commonwealth of Pennsylvania")
*'''Nickname''': one or more nicknames for the state, usually separated by semicolons

## Flag
*'''Flag''': file name of the state flag image; example: {{para|Flag|Flag of California.svg}}
** A small border is drawn around the flag except in the case of {{para|Name|Ohio}}, which is the only state with a non-rectangular flag.
*'''FlagAlt''': (''optional'') [alt-text](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/WP:ALT) for the flag image; if unspecified, the alt-text defaults to "Flag of <var>Name</var>"
*'''Flaglink''': (''optional'') a link to an article about the state's flag; if unspecified, the link defaults to "<nowiki>[Flag<nowiki>](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/</nowiki>Flag_of_<var>Name</var>)</nowiki>"

## Seal
*'''Seal''': file name of the state seal image; example: {{para|Seal|Seal of California.svg}}
*'''SealAlt''': (''optional'') [alt-text](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/WP:ALT) for the seal image; if unspecified, the alt-text defaults to "State seal of <var>Name</var>"
*'''Seallink''': (''optional'') a link to an article about the state's seal; if unspecified, the link defaults to "<nowiki>[Seal<nowiki>](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/</nowiki>Seal_of_<var>Name</var>)</nowiki>"

## Motto
*'''Motto''': (''optional'') state motto

## Song
*'''StateAnthem''': (''optional'') state song or anthem

## Map
*'''Map''': file name of a map highlighting the state; example: {{para|Map|California in United States.svg}}
*'''MapAlt''': (''optional'') [alt-text](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/WP:ALT) for the map image; if unspecified, the alt-text defaults to "Map of the United States with <var>Name</var> highlighted"

## Languages
*'''OfficialLang''': (''optional'') the state's official language(s)
*'''Languages''': (''optional'') language(s) with a significant number of speakers in the state; often listed with a percentage, such as "English 94.6%, Spanish 5.9%"

## Demonym
*'''[Demonym](1021-demonym.md)''': (''optional'') the name for residents of the state; in some cases editors have linked these terms to [List of demonyms for U.S. states](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_demonyms_for_U.S._states)

## Cities
*'''Capital''': the state's capital city
*'''LargestCity''': the largest city (by population) in the state
** Specify {{para|LargestCity|capital}} if the capital is also the largest city
*'''LargestMetro''': (''optional'') the largest [metropolitan area](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_metropolitan_areas_of_the_United_States) (by population) in the state

## Area
*'''AreaRank''': where the state ranks on [List of U.S. states and territories by area](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_area); example: {{para|AreaRank|5th}}
*'''TotalAreaUS''': land + water area in square miles (usually rounded to a whole number)
*'''TotalArea''': land + water area in square kilometers (usually rounded to a whole number)
*'''WidthUS''': width of the state in miles (usually rounded to a whole number or approximated to the tens place, such as "360")
*'''Width''': width of the state in kilometers (usually rounded to a whole number or approximated to the tens place, such as "580")
*'''LengthUS''': length of the state in miles (usually rounded to a whole number or approximated to the tens place, such as "360")
*'''Length''': length of the state in kilometers (usually rounded to a whole number or approximated to the tens place, such as "580")
*'''PCWater''': percent of total area that is water; example: {{para|PCWater|3.2}}
*'''Latitude''': range of latitudes in the state, usually given in degrees and minutes from the southernmost to the northernmost parallel
:example: {{para|Latitude|25° 50′ N to 36° 30′ N}}
*'''Longitude''': range of longitudes in the state, usually given in degrees and minutes from the easternmost to the westernmost meridian
:example: {{para|Longitude|93° 31′ W to 106° 39′ W}}

## Population
*'''PopRank''': where the state ranks on [List of U.S. states and territories by population](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_population); example: {{para|PopRank|25th}}
*'''2010Pop''': the most recent U.S. Census population for the state; most states list the most-recent population estimate
*'''2010DensityUS''': most recent population ÷ land area in square miles
*'''2010Density''': most recent population ÷ land area in square km
*'''2000Pop''': [2000 United States Census](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/2000_United_States_Census) population for the state
** Overridden by ''2010Pop''.
*'''2000DensityUS''': 2000 population ÷ land area in square miles
** Overridden by ''2010DensityUS''.
*'''2000Density''': 2000 population ÷ land area in square km
** Overridden by ''2010Density''.
*'''DensityRank''': where the state ranks on [List of U.S. states by population density](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_by_population_density); example: {{para|DensityRank|33rd}}
*'''MedianHouseholdIncome''': (''optional'') see [Household income in the United States](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Household_income_in_the_United_States)
*'''IncomeRank''': where the state ranks on [Household income in the United States](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Household_income_in_the_United_States)

## Elevations
*'''HighestPoint''': text naming the location of the highest point in the state; usually it's the name of a mountain or hill
*'''HighestElevUS''': elevation of the highest point in feet
*'''HighestElev''': elevation of the highest point in meters
*'''MeanElevUS''': mean elevation of the state in feet
*'''MeanElev''': mean elevation of the state in meters
*'''LowestPoint''': (''optional'') text naming the location of the lowest point in the state; usually it's the name of a body of water
*'''LowestElevUS''': elevation of the lowest point in feet
*'''LowestElev''': elevation of the lowest point in meters

## History
*'''Former''': (''optional'') the article name for the place before it became a state; example (for Oregon): {{para|Former|Oregon Territory}}
*'''AdmittanceDate''': the date the state joined the U.S.
*'''AdmittanceOrder''': the rank among [List of U.S. states by date of admission to the Union](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_by_date_of_admission_to_the_Union)

## Government
*'''Governor''': the name and party of the current governor
*'''Lieutenant Governor''': the name and party of the current Lt. Governor/next-in-succession
*'''Lieutenant Governor_alt''': (''optional'', defaults to "Lieutenant Governor") the name of the title that succeeds the Governor; several states have no Lt. Gov. and instead their Secretary of State or President of the Senate is next in succession
*'''Legislature''': a link to the article about the state's legislature; example: {{para|Legislature|<nowiki>[New Mexico Legislature](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/New_Mexico_Legislature)</nowiki>}}
*'''Upperhouse''': a link to the article about the state's lower legislative house; example: {{para|Lowerhouse|<nowiki>[Senate](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Wisconsin_State_Senate)</nowiki>}}
*'''Lowerhouse''': a link to the article about the state's lower legislative house; example: {{para|Lowerhouse|<nowiki>[State Assembly](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Wisconsin_State_Assembly)</nowiki>}}
*'''Senators''': the two U.S. Senators from the state, listed by name and party; the two are separated by a linebreak <nowiki><br /></nowiki>
*'''Representative''': (''optional'') text that appears before the link to the list of Congressional delegations
:Most states tally the number of Representatives per party (example: {{para|Representative|6 Democrats, 6 Republicans}}). States with only a few  Representatives usually list the people by name.

## Time zones
If ''TimeZone2'' is specified, the time zone section will consist of three rows:
:{|
| '''[Time zones](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_time_offsets_by_U.S._state)'''
| ''TZDesc''
|-
| -''TZ1Where''
| ''TimeZone''
|-
| -''TZ2Where''
| ''TimeZone2''
|}

Otherwise, only ''TimeZone'' should be specified (the other related parameters are ignored) and one row is rendered:
:{|
| '''[Time zones](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_time_offsets_by_U.S._state)'''
| ''TimeZone''
|}

## Other information
*'''ISOCode''': (''optional'') the [ISO 3166-2:US](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/ISO_3166-2:US) code for the state
*'''PostalAbbreviation''': (''optional'') the state's current standard [postal abbreviation](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_U.S._state_abbreviations)
*'''TradAbbreviation''': (''optional'') common traditional abbreviations for the state
*'''Website''': (''optional'') an official URL for the state
*'''Footnotes''': (''optional'') where footnotes can be placed
:As of 2013, only [Rhode Island](1039-rhode-island.md) uses this parameter.
