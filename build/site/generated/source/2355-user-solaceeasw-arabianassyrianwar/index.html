The '''Arabian-Assyrian War''' was an armed conflict between the Iranian-led [Islamic Security Council](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Islamic_Security_Council) and the Saudi-led [Arabian Military Organization](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Arabian_Military_Organization). The conflict lasted nearly ten years, from the 1 January 2064 to 5 November 2073.

Following the loss of Turkish military and political presence in the Middle East following [World War III](107-world-war-iii.md), the last remaining powers in the Middle East were Iran and the Arabian State. In 2060, Iran, along with it's allies Assyria and Lebanon, formed the Islamic Security Council to help establish dominance over the region through the process of federating the Middle East into a fundamental Shia-based Islamic Caliphate. In response to this, in 2061, the Arabian State would form the Arabian Military Organization under what remained of the Arab League, a military pact built on an emphasize of sovereignty and preserving the status quo through force. Border disputes and the consequences from World War III that were brought to the region would lead to militaristic mobilization on both sides.

The Arabian Military Organization would declare total war on Assyria on January 1, 2064, after evidence of state-sponsored revolutionaries in Iraq were leaked. Additionally, it had been argued that Assyrian President Gharib al-Tahari had been using chemical weapons on the Sunni-based Muslim population. Although these attacks weren't confirmed to have taken place, the Assyrian government was found to have been stockpiling chemical weapons, having received most of them from Iran. Deciding to take a pre-emptive attack against the Iraqi state before any further human rights issues were to be discovered, the Assyrian and Iranian governments would co-operatively launch an invasion the next day, nearly occupying the entire state within a couple of weeks. However, after the Arabian state's mobilization finished and their usage of chemical attacks to clear large portions of the front-lines, trenches would be dug throughout Iraq as forces dug-in for a war of attrition.

The major stalemates would occur from 2065-2069, ending with the [Assyrian Civil War](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Assyrian_Civil_War). This would ultimately collapse the Assyrian war effort, forcing the State to withdraw from the conflict. The loss of Assyria would be the final  nail for the Islamic Security Council, as Lebanon would drop out shortly after, leaving the Iranian government to begin peace negotiations after being pushed as far back as Esfahan and Shiraz. Assyria would additionally be invaded and occupied by the Arabian State. The final year of the war is described as "harsh" on the Middle East, as Iranian chemical attacks continued to bombard Arabian-occupied cities almost daily, with the average death count rising from 15,000 to nearly 100,000. Civilian casualties rose to over 1,400,000 during this time, which caused [an international reaction](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/International_response_of_the_Arabian-Assyrian_War). Ultimately, the Arabian Military Organization's goals would change after the relentless behaviour of the remaining Iranian government, resulting in an overall occupation of the Middle East in a joint effort with Turkey. 

The war is seen as one of the bloodiest military conflicts in [21st Century](94-21st-century.md), due to it's number of casualties of both military personal and civilians. It is also one of the few conflicts in the 21st Century that used "outdated" military tactics, including large-scale trench warfare, fortified military fortresses, and the use of chemical and nuclear weaponry, but the influence of new military technology provided a gateway for both sides to make larger advancements into either's territory, especially through tactical aerial bombing. The war has been largely compared to the Iran-Iraq War of the 20th century, in terms of military strategies used and the overall military mobilization.
