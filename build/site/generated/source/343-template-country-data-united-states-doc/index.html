## Unincorporated territory flags
{{mono|Country_data}} templates are also available for several organized [territories of the United States](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/territories_of_the_United_States). Many of the unorganized territories are either uninhabited or have no indigenous inhabitants, and also do not have unique flags. For those, please use <code><nowiki>{{flagicon|United States}}</nowiki></code>.
