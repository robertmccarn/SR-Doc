The 2060s was a decade spanning from January 1, 2060, to December 31, 2069. 

The 2060s were a period of recovery. Thanks to efforts of the Earth Working Group, the global climate began to rapidly cool, reversing the effects of the Flood decades before. By 2066, the ocean level was so low, it was at levels not seen since the Industrial Revolution. This changed the physical geography of the world yet again, as not only were the coastlines reclaimed, but new lakes in Africa formed to make the Sahara region no longer an uninhabitable desert. This geographical shift was known as the Refreeze. Also, the world began to grow out of the destruction of the Third World War. The United States saw an era of unprecedented power over the world, despite engaging in war. The 2060s also saw a new era in space travel, and contact with aliens was made for the first time in 2064. The warp drive, a revolutionary technology pioneered by American scientists, further allowed astronauts to explore the cosmos unlike previous eras of history. However, America's dominance, especially in space, was seen as tyrannical by other governments. 

Throughout the 2060s, the Japanese and Turkish spheres of influence continued to decline. Even though they were shut down in 2055 (following the aftermath of the Third World War), the two nations gradually weakened through this decade and beyond.  

# Events
## North America
2060: Start of the "Epical Period", a unique era of music which blended different sorts of rock music together. This music would become very popular during the 2060s.   

2063: Closure of the Northwest Passage. After opening up for trade with the Arctic region decades prior, this closed after the climate cooled. This cut off trade with the Arctic states, causing them to suffer economically. This would later prompt them to start the First Nations War.  

2064: Start of the First Nations War. Following the Refreeze, the economy of the Arctic crashed following the closing of the vital Northwest Passage. These desperate nations invaded Canada in the south, prompting America to intervene to protect American interests. Following American victory, Canada was put under the American jurisdiction. 

# Births
2066: Christopher Reagan, 63rd President of the United States
