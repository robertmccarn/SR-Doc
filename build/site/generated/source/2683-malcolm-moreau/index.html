    Damian Frederick Holland, or better known by his penname, as Malcolm Moreau, is a [theriform human](1450-human.md) science fiction writer and comic book artist. He is best known for his 10-book series entitled ''The Microcontinents: A Natural History''.

# Early Life
Damian was born on January 17, 2086, in Washington County, Oregon. As a theriform human, he had the physical characteristics, and abilities of the animal donor parent. In Damian's case, it was an emu, which made him a fast runner, and agile. Making any attempt at catching him a near impossible task. His emu DNA also gave Damian a shaggy appearance, which was often a source of ridicule. He was also known for having a relatively deep voice compared to the other kids.

At age 17, he became interested in speculative biology after acquiring copies of ''The World of Kong: A Natural History of Skull Island'' by Weta Workshop and ''After Man: A Zoology Of The Future ''by Dougal Dixon, as well as becoming an active member of the ''Speculative Evolution'' internet forum under the username Shagrat86. Despite the books' scientific inaccuracies regarding the wildlife, it sowed the seeds in his mind, the blueprints of what would become his life's work. During his writing class, he presented a rough draft of a story that he described as, "an example of how not to write a hollow earth story." Despite his lack of confidence in the draft, it was well-received by most of his classmates.

He graduated from Jefferson Online College with a modified diploma and received a PhD in Linguistics on June 14, 2110.

# Natural Histories
Holland's most famous work by far is ''[The Microcontinents: A Natural History](2810-the-microcontinents-a-natural-history.md),'' a 10-book series describing hypothetical ecosystems that may have existed in Earth's distant past on landmasses that have since disappeared underwater. Each book in the series was styled as a "field guide" to a different prehistoric landmass at a different time. The series currently contains ten books.

The books included the following:

''Tropical Antarctica ''(Antarctica, 150 million years ago)

''Edge of the World'' (Kerguelen Plateau, 75 million years ago)

''Sundaland (''Indonesia, 2 million years ago)

''The Great Island ''(Maui Nui, Hawaii, 1 million years ago)

''Shell, Feather, and Bone ''(Zealandia, 35 million years ago)

# Physical Appearance
As mentioned above, Damien is a genetic chimera of a human and an emu, with the balance being human. While he has a human's general body plan, the lower portions of his legs are elongated, and heavily muscled. Each foot is digitigrade in structure and bore three, long toes, with the middle toe being longer than the other toes. The structure of his feet meant that if he was in a tough situation, he could outrun his aggressors, or strike them down with his bone-cracking kicks. His arms were normal human arms, but with feather quills lining his elbows, and ended at the wrists.
