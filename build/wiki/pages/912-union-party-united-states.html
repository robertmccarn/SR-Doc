The Union Party was one of the major contemporary political parties in the United States and its Associated states.

Tracing its origins back to the founding of the Interplanetary Trade Commission, the Union Party was founded around 2088 in an effort to unite the colonial delegation to Earth under a single political apparatus. There have been 3 Unionist presidents, the first being Lionel Halvidar, who served from 2133 to 2141.
