Bavaria, officially the Republic of Bavaria, was a sovereign nation located in Central Europe that would end up participating in the Third Mexican-American War. Created after the dissolution of Germany, Bavaria bordered Saxony to the north, the Czech Republic to the east, Austria to the south, and Rhineland to the west. The main ethnic group of Bavaria were German's, with small populative pockets of Austrians and Czechs. The main religion is Catholicism and Christianity, with a plurality of Islamic and generally non-religious citizens. The official language of Bavaria is German.
