Rayleen Malakar (April 1, 2035 – 2144) was an American politician who was the 57th President of the United States from 2089 to 2093 and the 87th United States Secretary of State from 2085 to 2089. A member of the Horizon Party, she was previously a Senator, and Director of Central Intelligence.

In 2088, Malakar ran a successful campaign to succeed Lachman as President, winning a comfortable 55% of the popular vote. Foreign policy drove the Malakar presidency, as the Central American annexation crisis began just three weeks into her Presidency; an event that would ultimately lead to the Second Mexican-American War 1 year later. Domestically, Malakar presided over a weak recovery from the Second Depression, along with continuing budget deficits and the perceived defeat in the Second Mexican War, she lost the 2092 presidential election to Liberal Seras Stultz.
