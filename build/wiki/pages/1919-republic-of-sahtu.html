Sahtu was an independent sovereign country in the North American Arctic that existed until January 10, 2073. Sahtu was bordered by Yukon to the west, Tlicho to the south, and the Nunavut to the East. The native citizens of Sahtu were predominantly of Dene and Metis ancestry, however the bulk of the country's population were from the British Isles and the former Low Countries.

Overview[]

Sahtu was the wealthiest and most powerful of the First Nations. Perfectly situated along the Mackenzie river with wide harbors to move goods from the Northwest Passage to and fro from further south, Sahtu rose to wealth and prominence in the mid-21st Century. While power did rest in the wealthy chiefs on the Tribal Council, the country's population was largely made up for British, Australian, and Dutch climate refugees, with a small population of ex-Russian oil and gas workers.

Like all of the Arctic States, Sahtu's economy collapsed during the Refreeze and most of its population fled South, literally to greener pastures. With its economy in ruins, but still holding a not inconsiderable military force, Sahtu joined forces with the other Arctic states in a desperate bid to take hold of better lands further south to resettle its population and rebuild its economy rather than return to the life they'd known for so long prior to the Flood. Taking advantage of a weakened Canada with their own economic problems, Sahtu hoped to wage a brief skirmish to force the Canadians to a political settlement, however the invasion dragged on into a war that would eventually bring in the United States and see Canada and the First Nations under an American occupation.
