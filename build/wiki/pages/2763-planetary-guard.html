Planetary Guard

The United States Planetary Guard (USPG), was the administrative body for the militia forces of the interplanetary colonies. It was unique among the US military chain of command, in that it was organized under the Interplanetary Trade Commission rather than the Department of Defense. Whilst its loyalties were formally to the President of the United States, in reality the commander-in-chief of the Planetary Guard was always recognized to be the Chairman of the Interplanetary Trade Commission. In 2134, the Planetary Guard was formally dissolved as a unified militia organization and its forces and responsibilities were either rolled into the United States Space Force or transferred to the United States Space National Guard.
