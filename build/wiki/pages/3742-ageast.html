AGEAST

Army Group East, abbreviated as AGEAST and officially known as the 25th Army Group, was a United States Army army group formed on the east coast of the United States during the White Tide. Along with Army Group West (AGWEST), it formed a significant part of the Federal government's forces against the MAGA government's National Defense Force.

First Army[]

The First Army was engaged in battles in the Great Lakes areas and gained fame during the defense of Rock Island Arsenal and the rescue of besieged Chicago.

Third Army[]

Considered to be the backbone of AGEAST, the Third Army fought on the East Coast in the defense of Washington, D.C. and the Northeastern Green Zone. It was primarily made up of veteran enlisted soldiers and officers who had participated in the Middle Eastern wars of the 2010s.

Fifth Army[]

The Fifth Army became well-known for its defense of South Texas. However, it was primarily made up of noncombatant personnel during the start of the war in indefensible positions, leading to high casualties. The Fifth Army was bolstered by a significant allied partisan resistance movement in South Texas and avoided the severe winter of 2029. Units of the Fifth Army would be the first to discover the MAGA regime's death camps.
