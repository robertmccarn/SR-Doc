Bradbury Cooperative

The Bradbury Cooperative was a network of Martian colonies founded with the original Bradbury settlement. Based primarily in Western Tharsis, but including settlements in the Margaritifer basin and at other points across the planet, the Bradbury CoOp was the first example of genuine self-government on Mars that was not tied to a terrestrial entity.

The Bradbury CoOp proved to be a pivotal force during the First Martian Revolution and for this position the city of Bradbury would become the headquarters of the Interplanetary Trade Commission and later the formal planetary capital of Mars.
