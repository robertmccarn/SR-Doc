Rhineland,  officially the  Republic of Rhineland, was a sovereign nation located in Central Europe that would end up participating in the Third Mexican-American War.

History[]

Created after the dissolution of Germany, Rhineland bordered Hanover to the north, Saxony and Bavaria to the east, Switzerland to the south, and France and the Netherlands to the west. The main ethnic group of Rhineland were German's, with small populated pockets of French and Swiss. The main religion is Protestantism and Christianity, with a plurality of Islamic and generally non-religious citizens. The official language of Rhineland is German.
