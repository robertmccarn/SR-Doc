The 2056 United States Presidential Election was the 68th quadrennial presidential election. Democratic Party Candidate Dan Forest won the election thanks to having the majority of second and third choice votes.. This was the first election where a candidate with the most votes on the first ballot lost, leading to President Elect Dan Forest to promise only a single term as President.

Background[]

In the aftermath of World War III, the United States became split upon on the decision of what the post-war world would look like and be maintained. 

Nominations[]

Democratic[]

While respected as a competent and moderate Vice President, Forest was not President Jacobi's first choice to succeed her. After repeated attempts to push Secretary of the Space Force, James E. Knight to seek the Democratic nomination, the Progressive Caucus and President Jacobi endorsed Forest as the nominee. To the surprise of many in the party, Forest's laid back attitude struck a chord with the nation, exhausted by war, frozen conflicts, and economic upheaval.

Result[]

The results were surprising when Conservative candidate Brandon Tran's bipartisan strategy to win the Progressive and Conservative votes allowed him to gain enough votes to secure the majority of the first ballots. However, Brandon Tran's neglect of the lower parties had resulted in Dan Forest's domination of being in their second and third choices. It gave him enough power to ultimately win more than 51% of the the vote to narrowly steal the election.
