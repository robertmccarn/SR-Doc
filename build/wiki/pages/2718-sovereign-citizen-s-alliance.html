The Sovereign Citizens Alliance was an American Far Right terrorist organization and former unrecognized quasi-state that followed a fundamentalist Christian and White Nationalist doctrine commonly known as Trumpism. The SCA gained global prominence during the White Tide when it formed a pretender government over the "Red States" of the United States following the election of 2028.

The SCA received notoriety for its violence against non-white, non-protestant, non-neurotypical and non-cis/het peoples. It carried out executions against soldiers and civilians, including journalists and political activists, and destroyed centers of non-white heritage. Their reign of terror was gruesome, and highly destructive as it directly led to the American Famine of 2029-2030 through the supply chains they interrupted. All told, it is estimated that some 11 million Americans died as a result of the SCA's actions. The organization's surviving leadership were held responsible for numerous war crimes and crimes against humanity, and were all given the death penalty.

The SCA was the result of the Congress of Sovereign Citizens in 2026, in which several of the White Nationalist, Alt-Right, and neo-fascist groups that had been rising in the US declared the creation of a single organization and military leadership. The group proclaimed the restoration of the "American Republic" and began referring to itself as the Sovereign Citizens Alliance or the SCA in January 2028 after its declaration of rebellion and allegiance to the pretender government initially based in Palm Beach, Florida.

Purpose and strategy[]

Ideology[]

The SCA was a white nationalist, Christian fundamentalist group whose origins lay with the Sovereign Citizen, Alt-right, and Q-anon movements within the broader Conservative movement. While the SCA had elements of many different far right groups, the core leadership saw themselves as a Sovereign Citizens fighting to retake the United States in a Second American Revolution, although their Neo-Confederate supporters would often refer to their movement as the Third American Revolution. Sovereign Citizens of the 2020s believed that the United States effectively ceased to exist after the passage of the 14th Amendment in 1871, and the country from that time forward was led by a cabal of Satan-worshiping pedophiles.

Right-wing nationalist groups inside the Republican party argued that the SCA did not represent the principles of American nationalism and were not true conservatives, however few in the Republican Party would claim that they weren't true Christians. The SCA promoted religious violence, and regards Christians who do not agree with its interpretations as apostates or closeted atheists. Officially, the SCA never actually claimed to be a white nationalist or even Christian nationalist organization, but many of its leaders argued that White Men in the US were being oppressed and under threat of having their cultural identity being destroyed. Despite these claims, the bulk of the SCA's attacks were disproportionately targeted at people of Hispanic, African, Asian, Arab or mixed ancestry. The stated core of the SCA's ideology was traditional American republicanism, and the restoration of state's rights over federal power.

The Congress of the Sovereign Citizens, was originally convened in opposition to the emergency population relocation measured passed by the Harris administration which moved several million East Coast urbanites into rural communities, many of whom had remained in those states well past the time they were promised to return home. The result was the passage of progressive policies like comprehensive firearm background checks and increased representation for minority groups. The SCA interpreted this as the start of a culling by the cabal. Many units within the SCA flew some variation on the US Civil Flag, a banner normally adopted by anti-tax Sovereign Citizen groups for many years prior to the White Tide. Interestingly, Mike Wallace, the leader of the SCA and briefly self professed rightful President of the United States following the death of Donald Trump, claimed to be the 20th President of the United States, and that every President after Ulysses S. Grant was illegitimate, with the exception of himself and his predecessor.

Symbols and Flags[]

For the first 8 months of its life, the SCA had no official flag. The National Defense Force primarily utilized uniforms taken from the National Guard or State Defense Forces, and thus wore the flag of the United States. Several unofficial flags were used by states and municipalities loyal to the Maga government. The "Thin Blue Line" flag was often used by Police Infantry Units, while NDF units and militias often used either some variation on the Betsy Ross Flag, Trump's 28' campaign flag, the Gadsden flag, the old Confederate flag, along with assorted neo-Nazi flags including the actual banner of Nazi Germany. On September 2nd, 2029 the American Congress passed a provisional flag act, requiring units display a variation of the Bonnie Blue Flag that featured a blue field and an outlining red stripe framing a single star. This flag grew to be quite popular within the Maga hierarchy and was enthusiastically adopted as the symbol of the revolt.

Christian dominionism[]

The SCA largely subscribed to dominion theology, a Christian political ideology that sought to institute a nation which is governed by Christians based on an extreme right-wing interpretation of biblical law.

Organization[]

Palm Springs, Florida was under SCA control from 2028 where it became the group's de facto capital city. On 17 October 2029, following a lengthy battle that saw massive destruction to the city, the United States Marine Corps announced the full capture of Palm Springs from the SCA, by which time its leaders had abandoned the city. The SCA capital briefly relocated to Mobile before relocating to its final capital in the Great Plains.

Leadership and governance[]

The SCA officially recognized the Constitution of the United States, however did not recognize any amendment after the 13th Amendment as legitimate, and operated an extremely loose interpretation of Exception clause of the 13th amendment. Women and ethnic minorities had no right to vote or hold property and were treated as "subjects" not citizens. The SCA had no tax on income, income tax, capital gains, or estates, but instead used a national sales tax and voluntary contributions to fund its war effort.

Military[]

The Military of the SCA was referred to as the "National Defense Force" and was made up initially of defectors from the US military, police officers, and militia groups, however by 2030 it was overwhelmingly made up of fresh recruits, both volunteers and conscripts.

Civilians in SCA-controlled areas[]

In regions outside of frontline combat, social control of civilians was by imposition of the SCA's White Christian-nationalist ideology, enforced by Police infantry units. These units imposed "decency laws" passed by the SCA government and Maga state governments. These laws were often vague in their definitions and effectively gave cops the power to impose moral order however they saw fit. But in general civilians in SCA territory were prohibited from engaging in acts that violated gender binarism, neurotypical behavioral standards or heteronormative behavior. Church attendance was made mandatory, and ministers were encouraged to expose those that violated any social norms for either punishment by the state or mob justice.

While it styled itself a "Free Nation," citizens of the SCA were forbidden from forming labor organizations, and in effect were required to work from the direction of the state. Child labor laws were eliminated, and while most parents only permitted teenagers to go into the workforce or join the NDF, children as young as 5 years old in foster care were placed in factory and mining jobs.

Racial and Religious policies[]

Racism and Christian nationalism were basic tenets of the Republican Party and the SCA regime. The SCA's racial policy was based on white supremacy, while religious policy was based on Christian dominionism. The Maga government reintroduced anti-miscegenation laws, anti-sodomy laws, racial segregation and enforced anti-neurodiversity laws, and in some states non-whites were not even permitted to address whites without first being addressed by whites.

Genocide and Slavery[]

Shortly after the establishment of the SCA, its leaders authorized the mass arrest of transfolk, queerfolk, abortion providers, people who received abortions, and non-Christians. With rare exception, these people were sentenced to death. Initially housed in prisons, the victims of the SCA Christian nationalist policies were transferred to death camps and systematically executed by ICE agents.

Slavery was functionally reintroduced in the SCA, though it did exist within the United States prior to the White Tide as punishments for a crime. The SCA broadened the scope of forced labor and increased arrests, condemning all but the most outwardly loyal or submissive non-whites to slave labor. Political dissidents were almost always condemned to the same fate.

The goal of the SCA was the complete purge of initially all non-Christians from the country, and later its very specific list of sanctioned protestant faiths and eventually to amalgamate said faiths under a new denomination which would serve as a state religion. Slavery was to become the default form of punishment for crimes committed by non-whites. Fortunately, the SCA did not exist long enough to enforce its slave system and genocidal policies on the scale that it had intended. The first death camps and slave labor camps were discovered via satellite in spring of 2030 and by 2031 the largest of them had been liberated.

History[]

The SCA emerged as a serious threat during the Revolutions of 2027 when its forces successfully captured Montgomery and claimed the formation of the first Sovereign Citizens Republic. The capture of Montgomery emboldened many of their supporters and at the height of the Great Famine the SCA and its affiliates had captured several towns and small cities across Alabama, Mississippi, Georgia, Tennessee, Arkansas, Texas, Missouri, Kentucky, Ohio, and Indiana, clashing with National Guard forces and sustaining their grip on these communities by capturing truck stops and thus controlling the flow of food stuffs to much of their territory. The Harris administration responded by nationalizing the state national guards of the various states where SCA terrorist activity was most predominant, but refused to declare martial law. Harris's public response fueled opposition to the Democrats and the administration by Progressive groups who themselves were becoming emboldened by such a clear enemy to the Republic. While Harris's public response was seen as a half measure, the administration was also ordering covert operations against the SCA that would not become public knowledge until 2042. These operations were extremely effective in breaking the SCA's network of control, however the details of their battle record won't be declassified until 2177.

Despite the success of the US Special Forces, the SCA and their affiliates remained a serious public threat well into the year of 2028. Their territory had largely been pushed back to Alabama and Mississippi, but as they fell back the group and their supporters carried out increasingly violent acts of domestic terrorism, including setting fire to whole towns, destroying oil pipelines, and staging public executions of African-Americans, Hispanics, people of mixed race, Muslims, Jewish people, women, neurodiverse individuals, homosexuals, and reporters. While the strategic integrity of the SCA was collapsing, the brutality of these acts contributed to a public perception that the Harris administration was not capable of defeating the SCA and is cited by political theorists as an important contributing factor for the Progressive Sweep in the elections of 2028.

Shortly after Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez took office in 2029, the SCA had largely been pushed back to Mobile and their affiliates had gone underground. One of Ocasio-Cortez's first acts upon taking office was the deploying of the US Army to Mobile in a seven day assault that ended with the deaths of all remaining members of the SCA. Following the disbandment of the Sovereign Citizen's Alliance, the Ocasio-Cortez administration declared white supremacist groups and other domestic extremist movements to be a clear and present danger to the national security of the United States. Immediately after the liberation of Mobile, federal raids across the country against similar movements became commonplace and increased powers were given to the FBI to monitor and shut down online groups if they were determined to be a potential security threat. As a result of these acts, most white supremacist and racial extremist movements were forced back underground. The SCA was, and has been, the only attempt by these groups to form a unified political entity during this period.
