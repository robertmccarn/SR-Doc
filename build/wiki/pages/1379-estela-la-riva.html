Estela La Riva was the 60th President of the United States serving from 2105 to 2109. She was the first president to be elected and fully served in the 22nd century.

Presidency[]

Chinese Civil War[]

Main article: Third Chinese Civil War La Riva took office at the start of the Chinese Civil War and was responsible for the American peace keeping mission to Beijing. What was originally a very popular decision grew out of favor with the public and by the 2108 elections, La Riva took much of the blame for China's dissolution and the loss of so much of the country to Mexican influence.
