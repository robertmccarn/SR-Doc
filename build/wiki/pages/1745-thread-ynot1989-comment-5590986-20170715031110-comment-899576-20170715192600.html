You're mostly free to create whatever pages you like (I whole heartedly encourage users to create pages for dead links and especially figure out what hte hell's going wrong with some of the Templates), I just have final say on their content and can delete them if I so chose. 

Basically we just want pages that are consistent with what has been established in the timeline, and if something looks off I trust our other contributors to alert me to it if I don't already spot it in the Recent Activity Feed.

We are VERY careful about which real-world persons we name (I actually didn't come up with most of the names for the people in the Presidents list, so if it turns out that by coincidience or act of another editor from Future wikia or whatever I've named a real person we need to amend that). 

As for states on Mars, Jupiter, etc. you are allowed to make or fill them in, but I already have a sizable map of Mars (that I will finish some day so help me) and I try to keep the other bodies names based on areas that have already been named. So that is an area where I'll edit and delete with extreme predjudice.

The one thing I whole heartedly encourage people with photoshop skills to do is contribute images, but this is an area where I think people should check with me first so I can offer constraints or give a general, "Do whatever makes sense to you."
