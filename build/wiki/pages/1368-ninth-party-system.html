Ninth Party System

The Ninth Party System refers to the era of American national politics that began in 2132 with the rise of the Union Party under Lionel J. Halvidar. It followed the Eighth Party System which started under Oscar Lachman. It continued to the election of 2156 which saw the Union Party merge with the Progress Party which resulted in the Tenth Party System. The dominant personalities during this period are Presidents Lionel Halvidar (U), Ha-eun Zaya (U), two-time party nominee Anakin F. Johnson (U), and former Party leader Steven Shen (C).

The Ninth Party System was defined by a new form of American nationalism aimed at colonial integration. Economic policy was functionally replaced entirely by the Quantum Economic Model.

Conservative Party[]

Thang Levin's presidency had caused a schism in the Conservative party when many had blamed its cause for the Second American Civil War. The party would've collapsed if it were not for Steven Shen, who led the party during the early years of the Ninth Party system. The party allied with with more conservative elements of the Union Party and once the Second Civil War grew into the Third Mexican War. It had been credited to the creation of the doctrine of "New Globalism" which helped refined internationalism and was adapted to what would eventually become the American protectorate system. It became a formidable player in Earth-Commonwealth politics and continued to do so early into the Tenth Party system.

Liberacion Party[]

The sixth largest party in the United States that was formed during the 2144 Presidential Election and steadily grew into prominence by the 2146 midterm elections. It was formed as a merger of many minor parties in Latin America that had largely opposed American occupation and annexation of their countries, including the now largely unpopular Dignidad Party. It supported self-determination and preservation of foreign culture within the recently occupied and protectorate territories. It later became a symbol for those who had also opposed the rising American hegemony in countries outside of the United States during the era of the Ninth Party System.
