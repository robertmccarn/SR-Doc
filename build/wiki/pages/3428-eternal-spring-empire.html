Eternal Spring Empire

The Eternal Spring Empire was an unrecognized breakaway confederacy that existed for 20 days, from the 4th to 24th of December of 2149. The Empire comprised mainly of the Eternal Spring O'Neil colony ship and its confederated colonies that declared secession and warred against the United States during the Eternal Spring Revolt.
