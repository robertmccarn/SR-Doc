I don't deny that it could theoretically happen, but I'm going to take this up with YNot1989, since he's the one in charge of the site. 

Also, consider this. Germany today is deeply ashamed of the Third Reich, probably more than any other country. You can't show a swastika there unless it's for educational or artistic purposes, and all German children have to be taught about the atrocities committed by the Nazis. You can be fined for even saying "Hiel Hitler" in public. So if human cloning were ever made legal--especially cloning of humans from the past--I imagine that Germany would take major steps to keep potential sources of Hitler's DNA out of unscrupulous hands. Uniforms, weapons, medications, and things like that would all probably be locked up and closely guarded in case anyone tried to take DNA samples from them.
