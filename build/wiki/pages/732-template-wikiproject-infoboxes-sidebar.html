The {{WikiProject Infoboxes sidebar}} template is used on pages related to the WikiProject Infoboxes, without substitution, allowing the automatic inclusion of future modifications.

Usage[]

Place this sidebar template at the top of pages relevant to Infoboxes. The template is expanded by default.

The parameter collapsed may be used to collapse the sidebar listing and hide the detail.

{{WikiProject Infoboxes sidebar |collapsed}}
