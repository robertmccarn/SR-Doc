The United Congo Republic, more commonly known as Congo, is a country in Central Africa which borders the Atlantic Ocean to the west. By land area, Congo is the second-largest in Africa after the Tuareg Federation. The United Congo Republic is the most populous officially Francophone country in the world, and the national capital and largest city is Nkuna-Kinshasa, which is the nation's economic center.
