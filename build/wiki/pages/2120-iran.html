The Republic of Iran or Iran is an Associated state that fell under the influence of the United States in December 4, 2140 after the Third Mexican-American War. The United States provides defense, funding grants, integration into the Quantum Economic Model, and access to U.S. social services for citizens of these areas of Iran.

It was also the last country on Earth that became secularized.
