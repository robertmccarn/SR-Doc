I like this thought, however, take issue with what is percievably 'hostile' and intentionally malicious.  The thought of immigration and race dispersal through poverty alleviation efforts both come to mind.  When non-profit, often religious-based, organizations take a part in placing newcomers seeking asylum or displaced urbanites requiring low-income housing and cheap transportation, there is little harm in asking more racially homogenous and perhaps more prosperous counties or states to accept responsibility for these provisions.  One might even argue the willingness to accept a limited amount of responsibility in this particular arena entitles volunteers to more electoral votes once population increases enough.
