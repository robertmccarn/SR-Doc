So here's a little thought experiment that's been on my mind. Basically, place yourself in the shoes of a certain person at a certain point on the timeline, and come up with what sort of life they would have. For example, let's say. . .

I'm living in a coastal city during the Flood. What happens to me and my friends when the water reaches our homes?

I'm a harsh critic of President Jacobi's censorship policies during World War III, and attempt to publish an essay detailing my views. Am I put under government surveillance for this? 

I'm a Hispanic-American living in North Carolina during the Lachman Administration. I hate Lachman and the Deporatations. Am I declared a potential hostile because of this? 

I'm living in Maryland when the Mexican Space Command attacks Washington DC. Will I survive? <ac_metadata title="What If. . .?"> </ac_metadata>
