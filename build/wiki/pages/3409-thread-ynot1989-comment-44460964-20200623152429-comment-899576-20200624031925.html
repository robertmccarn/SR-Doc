Its taken me over a decade of work on this so that's a really hard question to ask.

At a high level, the timeline is heavily influenced by the works of George Friedman (a VERY imperfect writer, who happens to be one of the best forecasters out there), Jacob Shapiro and others of the greater STRATFOR, GPF community.

I pepper in climate change and space exploration in greater detail because those are two areas of my interest and expertese respectively. 

And there's lots of little stuff inspired from movies, anime, videogames, and of course actual history.
