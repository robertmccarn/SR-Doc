Olympus is a US state located in the western edge of the Tharsis Plateau on Mars. The state is named after Olympus Mons, the tallest mountain and also one of the largest volcanoes in the Solar System. The state is bordered by the Amazonian Sea to the west, Westylvania to the north, and the Tharsis Territory to the east and south.

Geography and Climate[]

Western Olympus is largely characterized by the plainlands south of Lycus Sulci. The central part of the state is dominated by the Olympian Escarpment, and the eastern part of the state is a mountainous area home to Mount Olympus. The state has a subtropical climate, and two unnamed rivers also flow across the state.
