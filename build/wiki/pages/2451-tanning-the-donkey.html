Tanning the Donkey

Tanning the Donkey: The Life and Death of the Democratic Party was created ten years in response to 'Skinning the Elephant', the book describes the series of circumstances that led to the rise of competing factions and the ultimate fracturing of the Democrat party after the fall of the Republican party. The book describes the Clinton Era to the last pitch of Julian Castro in 2032.
