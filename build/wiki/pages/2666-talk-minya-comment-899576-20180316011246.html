Minya can't have a fresh water ocean, the erosion of rocks by river systems will invariably lead to large bodies of water becoming salty. The Great Lakes are a rare exception because they sit on a high enough elevation that water being fed into them by rivers also feeds out of them downstream on the St. Lawrence to the Atlanitc Ocean, effectively prevening a buildup of Salt.

So, "No" to fresh water seas.

Second, global rainforests are a problem on a moon oribiting a gas giant. Let's say its as far away from its parent as Callisto is from Jupiter (and is thus clear of the radiation belt) and has a 17 day orbital period around the planet. So you'd have 8.5 days of darkness. The other 8.5 would have a normal day/night cycle as the moon spins on its access while exposed to the sun. So could such a lack of solar radiation exposer lead to rainforests? The planet is much more likely to defined more by scrubland, icy tundra and plants similar to those in the ocean that can survive with limited access to light.
