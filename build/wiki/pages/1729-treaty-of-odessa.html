Treaty of Odessa

The Treaty of Odessa, officially known as the Black Sea Security Treaty, was a treaty resulting from semi-secret talks that concluded in 2034 between Poland and Turkey, to which Japan and the United States assented. The agreement defined their mutually agreed spheres of influence and control in the territories around and within the former Russian Federation. The agreement was based on the premise that Poland and Turkey functionally controlled and would continue to control large swaths of the former Russian Federation and appropriate restrictions on the extent of that control should be created. The treaty, revealed to the public via the Associated Press and Al Jazeera on March 1, 2034, and its passage in the Turkish and Polish legislatures was sharply criticized for the lack of time offered for public debate.
