Well the ones I made up were just me randomly searching for names based on origin and mashing them together. In some cases I'd combine the first and last names of two world minor government leaders from the country in question. But a few of the Presidents names were created on Future.wikia by other editors, and I just never bothered to change them. A few names I'm fairly certain are literally those of their creators. But if they sounded cool I'd just let them go. I had a bad habit in earlier timelines of just creating continuationes of political dynasties. Meagan McCain and Malia Obama were Presidents in one iteration of the Second Renaissance. Thing is, you look at American history and dynasties don't have the kind of staying power we like to think they do. Only four sets of Presidents have ever shared a last name, and only two were close relatives. FDR was Teddy Roosevelt's fifth cousin for crying out loud. Anyway, so when a few new editors started changing Presidents names to reflect less of an emphasis on Dynasties I just let it go. I'm pretty happy with the current roster of names, and I try to make sure that administrations are referenced across multiple pages rather being isolated to a single era or event.
