Ir'ba

The Ir'ba are the only native intelligence to the planet Q'jo orbiting HD71334d. They are tall, slender and serpentlike. Posessing a narrow brain-case atop a narrow and extremely nimble spinal column, the Ir'ba posses only two actual manipulator limbs on the torso, and a serpent-like "tail" extending down the length of their body. Their planet is currently in the middle of the equivalent of an ice age, drastically increasing available land, when for most of their evolutionary history they were confined to marshlands and shallow seas, limiting the need for distinct lower limbs.

Society[]

The Ir'ba are divided into several paleolithic tribes across their planet's more temperate regions.
