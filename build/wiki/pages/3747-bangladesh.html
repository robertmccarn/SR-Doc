Bangladesh, officially the People's Republic of Bangladesh, was a nation in South Asia between 1971 and 2141, until it was incorporated into the United Indian Republic as part of U.S. President Lionel Halvidar's effort to establish associated states around the world. It was one of the most populous countries in the world, and among the most densely populated. In its post-Flood configuration it was bordered by India to the west, Tibet to the north, and Myanmar to the east.
