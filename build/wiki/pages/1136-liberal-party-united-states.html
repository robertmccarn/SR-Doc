The Liberal Party is one of the major contemporary political parties in the United States. The Liberal Party was founded in 2029 out of the merger of the Labor Party, the Working Families Party, and several former Republican splinter parties. The state-level wing of the Liberal Party currently holds majorities in 111 states.

For the bulk of their existence, the Liberals have been the United State's third largest party in Congress, typically aligning itself with the Libertarian Party and the Labor Party, as it did during the minority government of Thang Levin. Following the 2112 general election the Liberals were reduced to fourth place behind the Dignidad Party, a position it would maintain for most of the next two decades. In the 2132 general election under the leadership of Sarima Haden, the Liberals attached themselves directly to the Progress Party ticket, aligning with the Union Party for a new coalition government.

After the Third World War, the Liberals formed a new coalition with the Conservatives centered around neo-globalism. This would lead to a formation of the Horizon Party in 2070.
