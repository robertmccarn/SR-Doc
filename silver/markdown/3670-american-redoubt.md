The '''American Redoubt''' officially the ''Northwest American Republic'' ('''NAR''') and sometimes known as the '''Northwestern Front''' was a far-right proto-state based mostly in Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming.

The ideas of the Redoubt came from two sources: on top of the original American Redoubt idea intended on creating a community for conservative Christians, the Redoubt later took in ideas of the Northwest Territorial Imperative, which sought to create a White ethno-state in the same region. The flag of the Redoubt would later reflect this. Similar to other far-right rebel governments of the [White Tide](3593-white-tide.md), the American Redoubt was characterized by ultraconservatism and racism. Unlike some other far-right rebels, the American Redoubt was not based upon historical independence movements, and solely cared about creating a state for the White race, and not some geographic area (e.g. the South). Eventually, even after the federal government would eliminate most insurgents, violence would periodically break out in Idaho. Even after most of the White Tide ceased in the early 2030s, federal troops would later continue with rooting out insurgents from the Rocky Mountains until the fall of Salmon Idaho in 2035.
