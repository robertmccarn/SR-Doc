# Talk:Mars/@comment-29372813-20180726235149

## Preserved source

```wikitext
Perhaps instead of "Hyperdemocracy," Martian political culture values consensus building over simple Majoritarian Democracies they'd lived in on Earth. A trend toward more non-partisan politics, not that they won't have partisan divides, but living and growing in such a fragile place will naturally lend itself to more universal decision-making styles. This could explain why there was so much distaste for the MSIF while the First Martian Revolution was widely supported, the more naturally small-c conservative political culture was averse to such sharp change when only fraction of the citizens desire it. Maybe some Martian states and territories have formally non-partisan governments, examples being Canada's Northern Territories (excusing Yukon) and American Samoa, where the offices are legally non-partisan but of course politicians still have some National party affiliation.
```
