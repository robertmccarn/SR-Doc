'''Abram Kaplan''' was the commanding officer of the ISV-03 ''Constitution'' and defacto leader of the Confederation of Free States during the [Morak First Contact Wars](1286-squirrel-wars.md).

## '''Captain of the ''Constitution'''''
Kaplan's service during [World War III](107-world-war-iii.md) placed him in the running for the captain's chair of the ISV-01 ''Enterprise'', and many believed he was passed over for Jin Musgrave because [NASA](1592-nasa.md) wanted a civilian to be the first person to make First Contact, and Musgrave was an accomplished scientist as well as a commander of spacecraft.

## '''Actions on Tu'''
### '''Squirrel Wars'''
### '''Return to Earth and Investigation'''
Kaplan was severely reprimanded for his actions on Tu by NASA, but avoided court-marshal by the Space Force and was given a desk job at NASA Armstrong Space Center. In his autobiography, ''Contact: An Account of the First Interstellar War'' Kaplan defended his actions, but admitted that he felt the only reason he was not stripped of his rank and forced to resign in disgrace was because of the testimony of Commander Musgrave during the hearings.
