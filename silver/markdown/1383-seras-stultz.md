Stultz was the 3rd Governor of Cascadia, serving from 2089 to 2093. A member of the Liberal Party, and the first Jewish President, Stultz also had the distinction of being the first President from a former Canadian province (though Stultz was born in Nevada, and had been a US citizen all her life, she had lived in Canada a decade before annexation.) She became known for rather partisan politics which led to her defeat in 2096.

# Early Life and Career
## '''Early Life'''
Seras Stultz was born on August 1, 2045 to a local wealthy family in Carson City, Nevada. Her mother was a philanthropist and software engineer while her father was a director for several popular local films and documentaries. Growing up, Stultz was constantly taught that they "had everything" by her parents and was also subsequently shielded

Stultz attended Pioneer High School and graduated at the top 15% of her class in 2062. During the years of her time in highschool, she became one of the more popular students of her class. She first delved into politics in 2060 after supporting a Liberal candidate, but was unhappy with the contemporary politics after the 2060 US election of Conservative candidate Reed Chen. Stultz had believed in change, and claimed that she wanted to bring "people with likewise mindsets" into power to "make change".

She took advantage of her popularity by running for a school election during her last school year to local student council and closely won. This was her first experience in politics of any kind, and had ran into several problems during her early years as part of the student council. Stultz had several ideas in changing the system. It was noted that being "pampered" for all her life up to this point, Stultz suffered a mental breakdown. But had quickly recovered after stumbling across recordings of several speeches and written literature made by her "favorite person at the time"; Robert F. Kennedy. Inspired by Kennedy's actions, Stultz worked hard and became one of the more popular members of the student council during her time.

After Stultz graduated, she moved to Victoria, British Columbia in Canada to attend University of Victoria there.

# Presidency
## '''2092 presidential campaign'''
Stultz's 2092 presidential campaign was noted for her attempts to unite opposition parties against the ruling Horizon party, notably both Liberals and Progressives. She promised a marriage of both Liberal and Progressive ideals to help unite the Left. This strategy had allowed her to grab significant enough votes for a surprise Liberal party victory.

However, constant pressures from more traditional factions in her party had quickly made her backtrack on many campaign promises to progressive voters. This has generated backlash.
