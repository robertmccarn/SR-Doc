## Preserved source

{{WIP}}
Sophonts are intelligent beings with a base reasoning capacity roughly equivalent to that of humans, often known as Sapient or incorrectly as Sentient/Self-Aware life, as almost all complex organisms have a degree of self-awareness. A species does not need to have had produced a complex agrarian society or "civilization" in order to be considered a Sophont.

The following is a list of planets with intelligent lifeforms contacted by humanity who are generally accepted by the scientific community to be Sophonts.
