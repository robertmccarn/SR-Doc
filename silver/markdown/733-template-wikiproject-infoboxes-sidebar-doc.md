The '''{{tlx|WikiProject Infoboxes sidebar}}''' template is used on pages related to the [WikiProject Infoboxes](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Infoboxes), without [substitution](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/WP:SUBST), allowing the automatic inclusion of future modifications.

# Usage
Place this sidebar template at the top of pages relevant to Infoboxes. The template is expanded by default.

The parameter ''<code>collapsed<code>'' may be used to collapse the sidebar listing and hide the detail. 
: <code><nowiki>{{WikiProject Infoboxes sidebar |collapsed}}</nowiki></code>
