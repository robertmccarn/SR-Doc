The '''Liberacion party''', also known as the '''Liberationist Party '''(and colliquially known as the Liberationists), is a party that held its origins as a grand coalition of nationalist groups after the [Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md). The party advocates for political autonomy, traditionalism, separatism, and cultural preservation. 

The party ideological base was molded when many became concerned that global unification could lead to the extinction of certain cultures and ethnic groups that reside within their borders overtime. They generally argue that separate groups of people should be able to govern themselves independently or autonomously. By the rise of the Tenth party system, the Liberacion party had gradually shifted its definition into a party that advocates for multiculturalism and regionalism, fearing the decline of many cultures and languages around the world as a consequence of American expansionism. Outside of North America, many supporters of the party advocate for anti-expansionism. Their shift towards less radical policies had attracted many minor libertarian groups, particularly those who had an multiethnic background.They also oppose the establishment of Free States in planets of extraterrestrial species and preferred the alien species to be assisted to “ascend” to space at their own will rather than being absorbed through "cultural imperialism".

Unlike most parties, the Liberationist Party is more of a "big tent" party, composing itself of many factions united in a common goal. Although, the party holds more libertarian economic, social, and political values despite having groups who think otherwise.

The party has enjoyed popularity around recently annexed regions of the [United States](217-united-states-of-america.md) (such as Latin America) and enjoyed greater success outside the country in the associated states (such as the European Association).    

# History
The Liberacion Party was formed during the 2144 Presidential Election and steadily grew into prominence by the 2146 midterm elections. It was formed as a merger of many minor parties in Latin America that had largely opposed American occupation and annexation of their countries, including the now largely unpopular [Dignidad Party](273-political-parties-in-the-united-states.md). It supported self-determination and preservation of foreign culture within the recently occupied and protectorate territories. It later became a symbol for those who had also opposed the rising American hegemony in countries outside of the United States during the era of the [Ninth Party System](1368-ninth-party-system.md).

By the Tenth party system, the party became less radicalized as the party took on a more 'American-friendly' policy to attract voters. It still opposes American hegemony advocates for political autonomy, in which separate groups of people should be able to govern themselves independently or autonomously. They now generally prefer global co-operation not in form of a single united world government but rather large groups of people having equal sovereignty and reputation to voluntarily form a world organization instead, similar to the UN.
