'''Brandenburg, '''<span style="font-weight:400;">officially the</span>''' Republic of Brandenburg'''<span style="font-weight:400;">, was a sovereign nation located in Central Europe that would end up participating in the </span>[Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md)<span style="font-weight:400;">. Created after the dissolution of Germany and was seen as a the true successor to the former state, Brandenburg bordered Denmark to the north, Poland to the east, <span style="font-weight:400;">Saxony to the south, and Hanover to the west</span>. The main ethnic group of Brandenburg were German's, with a small populative pocket of Polish. The main religion is Protestantism and Christianity, with a plurality of Islamic and generally non-religious citizens. The official language of Brandenburg is German. </span>
