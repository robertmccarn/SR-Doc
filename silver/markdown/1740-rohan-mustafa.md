'''Rohan Mustafa''' (26 January 2079 – present) was an American five-star general of the Space Force and Supreme Commander of Planetary Task Force Pacific during the Third Mexican War. He was served as Secretary of Defense under Cora Fabian's administration and played a prominent war in organizing the post-war reconstruction of the Americas. He received the Medal of Honor for his service in the Texas Campaign, and was one of only three people ever to rise to the rank of General of the Space Force.

Mustafa was the leading US authority on assault carriers, serving aboard an assault carrier during his early years, and then later was key in the Planetary Guard's adoption of assault carriers designed to engage in combat with large stations that proved essential during the Belt Pirate Wars and the Third Mexican War.
