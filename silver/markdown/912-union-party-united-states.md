The '''Union Party''' was one of the major contemporary political parties in the United States and its [Associated states](2002-associated-state.md).

Tracing its origins back to the founding of the [Interplanetary Trade Commission](216-interplanetary-trade-commission.md), the Union Party was founded around 2088 in an effort to unite the colonial delegation to Earth under a single political apparatus. There have been 3 Unionist presidents, the first being [Lionel Halvidar](212-lionel-halvidar.md), who served from 2133 to 2141.
