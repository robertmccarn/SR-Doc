# Seikarte

## Preserved source

```wikitext
The '''Seikarte''' are the only [intelligent lifeforms](3723-list-of-sophonts.md) native to the planet Seika. At time of First Contact in 2071, the most advanced societies on the planet were organized into soilworks building heading communities, with a limited form of agriculture used to cultivate fodder for livestock and a type of highly fibrous or "wood" grass-like reed used in both construction and fabric. The Seikarte are one of the first Sophonts to be popularly labeled a "Wood Age" civilization, a term used to describe the bulk of post-paleolithic societies observed by humanity.
```
