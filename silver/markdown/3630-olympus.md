# Geography and Climate
Western Olympus is largely characterized by the plainlands south of [Lycus Sulci](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Wikipedia:Lycus_Sulci). The central part of the state is dominated by the Olympian Escarpment, and the eastern part of the state is a mountainous area home to Mount Olympus. The state has a subtropical climate, and two unnamed rivers also flow across the state.
