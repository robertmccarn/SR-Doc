```wikitext
# Mexican Crisis
Personally, I believe that the situation in Mexico will have to get worse before the government will take any serious action. Thus, an idea I have is the Mexican Crisis.

Sometime between the presidencies of Saul Rodriguez Calderón and Maria Vasquez Salazar, I personally believe there should be some sort of civil war in Mexico. This civil war would probably consist of the the secession of several regions (Yucatan and Chiapas?) and some sort of splinter government (Backed by the US?) in Northern Mexico. This civil war could last for about a decade before the central government is able to quell the rebellions. This could lead to a resentment of the US and lay the groundwork for not only Renacido but also the Mexican-American Cold War.
```
