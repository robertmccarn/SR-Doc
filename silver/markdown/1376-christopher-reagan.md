Christopher Reagan was the 62nd President serving from 2117 to 2121. He was a descendant of Ronald Reagan, the 40th President of the United States .
# Personal Life
## Early Years
Christopher Reagan was born in Colorado in 2066 to his parents Richard (who was the grandson of Michael Reagan, Ronald Reagan's adopted son) and Irene Reagan.
