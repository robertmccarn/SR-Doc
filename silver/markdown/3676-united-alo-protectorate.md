The '''United Alo Protectorate''' or '''UAP''' is a supranational and intergovernmental military and trade confederation of the [Argommi](1749-argommi.md). It is nominally centered on [Alo](3673-alo.md)'s southern continental masses of Osomi and Haklo, however it is in the process of colonizing other planets and moons of its star system.
