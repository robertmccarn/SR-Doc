# Bradbury Cooperative

## Preserved source

```wikitext
The '''Bradbury Cooperative''' was a network of Martian colonies founded with the original [Bradbury](2150-bradbury.md) settlement. Based primarily in Western Tharsis, but including settlements in the Margaritifer basin and at other points across the planet, the Bradbury CoOp was the first example of genuine self-government on [Mars](318-mars.md) that was not tied to a terrestrial entity.

The Bradbury CoOp proved to be a pivotal force during the [First Martian Revolution](1680-first-martian-revolution.md) and for this position the city of Bradbury would become the headquarters of the [Interplanetary Trade Commission](216-interplanetary-trade-commission.md) and later the formal planetary capital of Mars.
```
