'''Rhineland, ''' officially the ''' Republic of Rhineland''', was a sovereign nation located in Central Europe that would end up participating in the [Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md). 

# History
Created after the dissolution of [Germany](1421-germany.md), Rhineland bordered [Hanover](2209-hanover.md) to the north, [Saxony](2325-saxony.md) and [Bavaria](2326-bavaria.md) to the east, [Switzerland](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Switzerland) to the south, and France and the Netherlands to the west. The main ethnic group of Rhineland were German's, with small populated pockets of French and Swiss. The main religion is Protestantism and Christianity, with a plurality of Islamic and generally non-religious citizens. The official language of Rhineland is German.
