## Preserved source

```wikitext
Hm. The US, preoccupied with domestic reforms, was leapfrogged by China's military/space/cyber capacity. China took effective control of all East Asia markets except Japan, and drastically reduced needed parts supplies to the US. Worse, China took effective control of the international satellite network. The shift and its results were so sudden that most market watchers were caught sleeping. China sold US treasuries, peeled India and Europe away from the US economic orbit, and applied merciless tariffs to trigger a permanent reduction in US prosperity. The stock market lost 60% of its value and unemployment exploded.

An indebted US spiraled into bankruptcy and Weimar style inflation. Social services were drastically reduced provoking chaos in cities, and martial law was frequently imposed. The US and China were in the process of trading places.
```
