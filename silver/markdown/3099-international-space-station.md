# International Space Station

## Preserved source

```wikitext
The '''International Space Station''' ('''ISS''') is the oldest inhabited [Orbital](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Orbital) built by humans. Its first component was launched into orbit in 1998, with the first long-term residents arriving in November 2000. It has been inhabited continuously since that date. Development and assembly of the station continued well into the mid 21st century, with the platform undergoing its most extensive expansions in 2029 and in 2057.

When it was completed the ISS was the largest human-made body in LEO and was the first human made object visible to the naked eye. In 2029 the platform was moved from LEO to its permanent position in GEO.

The ISS has held many roles during its life, from international research lab, tourist attraction, interplanetary staging area, before transitioning to its current role as neutral negotiating position for both Earth's nations and alien cultures. Most of the laboratory space was converted into an international and eventually interspecies university after 2057, and the bulk of the platform serves a variety of diplomatic functions to this day.
```
