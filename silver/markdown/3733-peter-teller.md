# Peter Teller

## Preserved source

```wikitext
'''Peter Peneus Teller''' (10 November 1967-December 2149), later known as '''Don Pedro de Alta California,''' was an entrepreneur, venture capitalist, and political activist who played a leading roll in the [Neo-Jeffersonian movement](2813-neo-jeffersonianism.md) before siding with [Mexico](1391-mexico.md) in the [Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md). An important figure in the Digital Revolution of the late 20th, early 21st centuries, Teller spent most of the mid-21st century in New Zealand before returning to the United States during the [Biorobotics boom](2618-biorobotics-boom.md). He would later, famously, renounce his American citizenship and publicly endorse [Edwin Jaso](1444-edwin-jaso.md)'s war-effort, being elevated as a ''Don '' and member of the Mexican Senate in the process. 

Near his 100th birthday Teller recognized the opportunity from the unemployment crisis spawned by biorobots and [AIOSs](1236-artificial-intelligence-operating-system.md). With more people starting to suggest ending pro-immigration policies of the Progressive Era to curtail the problem caused by automation, Teller returned to the US and became an early backer of many Neo-Jeffersonian political groups, and [Oscar Lachman](896-oscar-lachman.md) in particular. Riding high on renewed popularity following his support for [Augment Rights](1074-augment-rights-movement.md), the nascent Horizon Party pushed him as an Elder Statesmen of the anti-Progressive alliance  emerging around the world. He became the first of these self-ascribed "Yeoman Capitalists" to privatize an entire city, that being Fort Collins, CO, which by 2073 was bankrupt and facing a decade long population flight. Teller, with the state government's blessing as a pilot project, established a corporation that directly oversaw the administration of the city with a robotic workforce. After only one year then-Governor Lachman successfully pushed to implement Neo-Jeffersonian civics policies across the state based on the apparent success in Fort Collins. When Lachman eventually became President, Teller rose to become one of the biggest land-owners in the US, directly administering cities across the world, with San Jose being his crown jewel.

Teller was one of the few people to loudly oppose the Rosiland Amendment, which allowed green card holders and other immigrant workers to travel to [Mars](318-mars.md) rather than be fully deported. He lived as one of the most powerful men on Earth right up until the election of [Lionel Halvidar](212-lionel-halvidar.md) and the Union Party sweep in 2132. Teller, despite his personal racism, sided with the [Aztlan movement](1681-aztlan-war.md) and Mexico to maintain his grip on power. During the Secession Crisis of 2132, he attempted to supersede the elected government of Colorado to bring the state into the [United States of Aztlan](948-united-states-of-aztlan.md). Most observers assumed Teller would succeed, given his overwhelming control over much of the state at that time. But Halvidar was quick to use the conventional military to rout Teller's force of mercenaries before they could get organized.

Teller retreated to San Jose, and eventually Mexico City where he publicly endorsed Edwin Jaso, renounced his American citizenship, and converted to Catholicism for which he was made "Don Pedro de Alta California." After a brief term as military governor of California, Teller fled North America for one of his last personal holdings, a small [O'Neil Ship](1738-o-neil-ships.md), the ''New Darmstadt'' that originally cycled between [Earth](272-earth.md) and the [Ahuiateteo Orbitals](1477-orbitals.md). Teller and a band of retainers took the ship on a highly elliptical orbit into Trans-Neptunian Space. Teller, no longer stylizing himself as Don Pedro, and instead having adopted the title '''Autarch of New Darmstadt'''
joined his ship with the [Eternal Spring Revolt](1219-eternal-spring-revolt.md), and at 182 was killed during the Space Force's raid on the rebel colonies.
```
