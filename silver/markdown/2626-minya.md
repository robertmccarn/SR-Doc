# Minya

## Preserved source

```wikitext
Minya is a habitable moon in the Gliese 581 (also known as The Velmen) System.  It orbits a gas giant planet that orbits a red dwarf smaller than Sol system's sun.  It's about the same size as Ganymede, if not slightly larger.

# Climate
Due to its size (about one-third the size of Earth) and low gravity, Minya is a world with a low-gravity, low-energy ecology. Because it is a moon rather than a planet, it spends much of its time eclipsed behind the planet it orbits, so a great deal of its time is spent in complete darkness. Local life forms have therefore had to evolve to cope with long "bright" and "dark" seasons not unlike those found near Earth's polar regions.     

# Fauna
As a low-gravity world, the majority of Minya's wildlife tend to have a gracile body plan and the largest "animals" on the moon can grow to sizes comparable to that of Earth's sauropods. 

# Flora
The Flora of Minya ranges from giant tree-like organisms to grass-like "plants" that make up a majority of the planet's surface. Unlike Earth's plants, the "plants" of Minya possess a circulatory system with a heart, allowing them to better metabolize nutrients in their world's low-energy environment.[Category:Moons](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Moons)
```
