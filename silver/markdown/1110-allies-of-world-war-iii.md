The '''Allies of World War III''', were the countries that together opposed the [Coalition forces](1438-eurasian-coalition.md) during the [Third World War](107-world-war-iii.md) (2051–2055). The Allies promoted the alliance as seeking to stop Turkish, and Japanese aggression.

The leaders of the "Big Four" – the United States, Poland, China, and Britain – controlled Allied strategy; relations between Britain and the United States were especially close.
