'''Horace Fadel''' (2029 – 2144) was an American politician who served as the 55th President of the United States from 2077 to 2081 during the Second Depression. A Progressive, as Secretary of Labor in the 2060s he introduced [AIOS](1236-artificial-intelligence-operating-system.md) systems into labor management boards to increase the efficiency of hiring practices and provided government support for the adoption of post-war labor saving technologies. As president from 2077 to 2081, his ambitious programs were overwhelmed by the Second Depression, which seemed to get worse every year despite the increasingly large-scale interventions he made in the economy. He was defeated in a landslide in 2081 by Horizon Party candidate [Oscar Lachman](896-oscar-lachman.md), and spent the rest of his life as a progressive denouncing right-wing policies, as Horizon politicians repeatedly used his Depression record to attack progressive and justify the reforms to the economy.


# Legacy
Fadel became a progressive spokesman in opposition to the domestic and foreign policies of the Horizon party. He opposed Lachman's deportation programs and the [Second Mexican-American War](821-second-mexican-american-war.md). He had better relations with President [Seras Stultz](1383-seras-stultz.md) , and Fadel helped produce a number of reports that changed U.S. occupation policy in Panama. By the time of his death in 2144, he had rehabilitated his image. Nevertheless, Fadel is generally not ranked highly in historical rankings of Presidents of the United States.

Many historians had compared his presidency to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Hoover Herbert Hoover]. Citing that both Fadel and Hoover's rise to power and were both very similar and is just another example of the repeat of history.
