'''Al-Hasa''', officially the '''Kingdom of Al-Hasa''', was a sovereign Arab state in the Middle East and Western Asia, that participated in the [Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md). Al-Hasa was bordered by Kuwait and Nadj to the north, Muscat and Abu Dhabi to the east, Aden to the south, and Asir to the west. Al-Hasa is recognized as the successor state to Arabia and the original Saudi Kingdom, with it's royal family and titles consisting of descendents of the Saudi family and related figures. The official language of Al-Hasa was Arabic, with English being regarded as a secondary language. The official religion was Sunni Islam, while Chrisitanity and Shia Islam followers making up less than 10% of the population.
