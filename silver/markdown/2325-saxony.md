'''Saxony, '''officially the''' Republic of Saxony''', was a sovereign nation located in Central Europe that would end up participating in the [Third Mexican-American War](119-third-mexican-american-war.md). Created after the dissolution of Germany, Saxony bordered Bandenburg and Hanover to the north, Poland to the east, Bavaria and the Czech Republic to the south, and Rhineland to the west. The main ethnic group of Saxony were German's, with small populative pockets of Polish and Czechs. The main religion is Protestantism and Christianity, with a plurality of Islamic and generally non-religious citizens. The official language of Saxony is German.
