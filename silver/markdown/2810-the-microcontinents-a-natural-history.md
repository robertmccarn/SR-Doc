# The Microcontinents: A Natural History

## Preserved source

```wikitext

[Category:Books](https://second-renaissance.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Books)
''The Microcontinents: A Natural History ''is a 10-book series by [Malcolm Moreau](2683-malcolm-moreau.md), that describe hypothetical ecosystems that may have existed in Earth's distant past on landmasses that have not left any fossil record. Each book in the series was styled as a "field guide" to a different prehistoric landmass at a different time.
```
