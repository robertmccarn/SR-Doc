# United Progressive Party (United States)

## Preserved source

```wikitext

The '''United Progressive Party''', colloquially known as the '''New Progressives''' or the '''UPP''', is a political party in the United States. They are also called '''UniProgs,''' typically by opposing factions. It is positioned on the Left of the American political spectrum. The party's current leader starting in 2156 is [Dom Terragno](1394-dom-terragno.md), the first President of the United States to run as a New Progressive. The party is usually differentiated from the Old Progressives in that they have fully embraced [Postnaturalism](1215-postnaturalism.md). 

In 2153, the American [Progress](136-progress-party-united-states.md) and [Union](912-union-party-united-states.md)Parties, as well as several minor parties in the newly admitted states and Commonwealths, merged, forming the United Progressive Party. The unified New Progressive Party generally favors expanding the role of the [Quantum Economic Model](1337-quantum-economic-model.md), a strong central government, centralizing the powers of federal government, while keeping those powers limited to what the Planetary governments cannot immediately handle.
```
