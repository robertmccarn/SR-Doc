# Arya Kanzaki

## Preserved source

```wikitext
'''Arya Kanzaki''' was a space explorer, businesswoman, and territorial governor, who famously lead the first human expedition to [Saturn](1426-saturn.md). Born in Hawaii to Japanese-American parents, she grew up in California and went into the oil and gas industry during the Shale-Gas Boom. She sold most of her assets on Earth to finance her trip to Mars in 2027.

Kanzaki led the Titan-1 Expedition of 2036 to 2038 on a 400 day mission from Mars to the Saturn system, and claimed the Titan for her company Titan Corp. Before the expedition, she was an extraction engineer at the Bradbury colony. After founding Titan Corp, Kanzaki would go on to become one of the wealthiest individuals in history, with exclusive rights to the sale of Nitrogen system-wide. She would go on to serve as a founder of the [Interplanetary Trade Commission](216-interplanetary-trade-commission.md).
```
