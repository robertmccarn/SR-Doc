The '''2056 United States Presidential Election ''' was the 68th quadrennial presidential election. Democratic Party Candidate Dan Forest won the election thanks to having the majority of second and third choice votes.. This was the first election where a candidate with the most votes on the first ballot lost, leading to President Elect [Dan Forest](1265-dan-forest.md) to promise only a single term as President.
# Background
In the aftermath of [World War III](107-world-war-iii.md), the United States became split upon on the decision of what the post-war world would look like and be maintained.

# Result
The results were surprising when Conservative candidate Brandon Tran's bipartisan strategy to win the Progressive and Conservative votes allowed him to gain enough votes to secure the majority of the first ballots. However, Brandon Tran's neglect of the lower parties had resulted in Dan Forest's domination of being in their second and third choices. It gave him enough power to ultimately win more than 51% of the the vote to narrowly steal the election.
