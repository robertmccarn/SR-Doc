# Europans

## Preserved source

```wikitext
'''Europans, '''taxonomically ''Homo cetimorphis, ''are a recently recognized offshoot of Mermaid, a subspecies of ''Homo Sapiens,'' inhabiting the subsurface oceans of Europa. Many scientists say that the genetic gap became so wide that Europans can be classified as a separate species altogether. Approximately 40,000 individuals have been identified, though only a third are estimated to have been born on Europa. Europans are the result of heavy genetic modification of humans, more genetically distinct than Mermaids or other [Human](1450-human.md) subspecies.

# Anatomy
Europans are often described as being similar in appearance to terran Baluga whales. Their bodies are not much larger than their Human ancestors, and feature a layer of blubber that provides insulation from the extreme cold of the Europan seas. Their bodies are covered in luminescent stripes and their eyes are 30% larger than normal humans, adapted for the extreme dark of Europa's seas. Their skin is pale white due to the lack of sunlight. They posses gills in place of lungs and survive off of introduced species of fish. Europans also lack body hair and head hair, as they would cause drag, and would make it easier for predators to snatch them up ease.

# Society
At least two Europan "settlements" have been identified, on the ocean floor which include several igloo like habitats with the capacity to provide additional heat and light to their occupants, and store sources of food. They are currently classified as 'Sentient', and are currently debating whether or not to rejoin American society. Europans were born out of the need to colonize the moon for its vast amount of water that was in high demand during the events of The Flood.  

Due to their lack of hair, they often wear blue colored headdresses tipped with luminescent beads as a replacement, and are very popular amongst their womenfolk, according to a few observations.  Other than this, they are usually unclothed.
```
