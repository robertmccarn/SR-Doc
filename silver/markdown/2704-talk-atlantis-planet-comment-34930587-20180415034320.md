```wikitext
The Nemo Station Incident '''NEEDS TO GO'''. It just isn't biologically possible. Even on Earth, most viruses, bacteria, and parasites only affect a single species or a few related ones, and so-called "zoonoses" have generally only been a big deal amongst livestock, which we've intentionally cultivated in close proximity for ages. Most other parasites are host-specific, and can't survive in a host that they haven't specifically evolved to feed on. For example, the parasitoid wasp ''Cotesia glomerata'' lays its eggs exclusively in the caterpillars of the cabbage white butterfly. It cannot suddenly make the switch to attacking humans. A creature from a completely different planet--one far more different from us in terms of biochemistry and nutritional requirements than the aforementioned wasp--would have even more difficulty doing that. 
```
