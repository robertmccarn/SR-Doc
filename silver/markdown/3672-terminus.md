# Terminus

## Preserved source

```wikitext
'''Terminus''' is the 9th Planet from the sun and the farthest known planet in the [solar system](1583-sol-system.md). The planet is named after [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminus_(god) Terminus, the roman god of borders.]

# Moons
Terminus is a Super Earth, 10.14 times more massive than Earth, and 2.77 times the size. It has 11 satellites, most of which are in highly elliptical orbits and believed to be captured Kuiper Belt Objects. Its two largest moons, Sabine and Titus, have diameters of 905 km and 1311 km respectively. Due to the extreme distance from the Sun, and relatively limited economic value, Terminus only has a population of a few thousand researchers and engineers. 

## Titus
Titus is the second largest moon of Terminus and its largest population center. Titus is cryosphere comparable to Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, or Enceladus with a small subsurface ocean. While most residents of Terminus live on its [Orbitals](1477-orbitals.md), the presence of liquid water in the subsurface ocean of Titus makes it the most important center for economic activity.

## Sabine
Sabine is the largest and most massive moon of Terminus. Sabine is slated to be deorbited down system to [Venus](1154-venus.md) to serve as its new moon, via one of the most ambitious [gravity propulsion projects ever attempted.](3656-gravitonics.md) There is an ongoing debate to rename Sabine "Cupid" in honor of its future position.
```
