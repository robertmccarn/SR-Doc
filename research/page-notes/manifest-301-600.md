### Page 310 — Conservative Party (United States)
The infobox gives a founding date of 1 February 2033 and says the party has been known as the Conservative Union (CU) since 2073. The prose traces its roots to the second National Union Party and the Republican Party's collapse during the White Tide; it describes formation during the 2030s, while the history says the first public meeting using the CU name occurred on 1 February 2073 at the San Mateo event center. The party's national breakthrough is tied to the Second Depression and Oscar Lachman's 2080 election. Thang Levin's 2128 unity ticket with Dignidad later fractures amid southwestern violence and Aztlan secession. Steven Shen leads the party 2133–41, develops "New Globalism," and repositions it during the Second American Civil War/Third Mexican War. The party allies with Libertarian and Liberal parties in 2148; Libertarians disband in 2151 and members move into the Conservatives; a Conservative–Liberal coalition forms by 2156. The 2160s UPP challenge is framed as a possibility, not a completed outcome. Active faction headings (Shen coalition/Reformist, Traditionalist, Libertarian, Centrist, Populist) have no substantive text; historical faction entries include Alt-Right, Third Way Democrat, Neoconservative, Neo-Stalwart, Neo-Jeffersonian, and Union Conservative.

### Page 317 — Evalyn Jacobi
Evalyn Ramona Jacobi (26 October 1991–15 November 2059) is identified as the 50th U.S. president, serving 2049–57; previously Washington Attorney General 2032–41 and Governor 2041–48. She wins the 2048 election with 55% of the popular vote. Her presidency is dominated by the approach to and conduct of World War III. The Thanksgiving 2051 Japanese "pebble-mob" attack disables U.S. orbital command platforms and kills 349 servicemen and 54 contractors; subsequent worldwide attacks raise the stated death toll to 7,149. The article describes her 2052 creation of the U.S. Space Force, later absorption of the Air Force, and the Orbital Recon Marines' reorganization. Second-term home-front mobilization includes the Military Production Agency, Strategic Resources Administration, National Guard anti-missile batteries, and micro-bond financing. Jacobi's postwar policy rejects reviving the UN and favors regional blocs and a ban on non-U.S. military activity in space. She is killed by an unexploded Turkish roadside bomb in Tehran in 2059. The legacy section explicitly records wartime suspension of habeas corpus, broad communications/data-mining, near-nationalization of media, and AI censorship with Congressional approval, alongside her reputation as the leader who secured victory.

### Page 318 — Mars
The 35,773-character article covers planetary geography, terraforming, settlement, political revolutions, demographics, religion, government, culture, and moons. It says Mars achieved human-rated habitability in 2081 after 50 years of terraforming, and calls it the second planetary commonwealth admitted to the Union. Settlement history begins with Mars Corporation's formation in 2023, the first crewed landing at Terra Meridani/Meridian Base in 2026, LE's Bradbury base, SpaceX's Opportunity, Heinlein/Tharsis in 2027, and Elon's Purchase at Sagan in 2028. Terraforming began with engineered extremophytes, comet/asteroid redirection, and later algae, fungi, plants, trees, insects, corals and plankton; NASA's Green Magnetoshield is described as a 2-Tesla field generator at Mars–Solar L1, mandated in 2033. The First Martian Revolution culminates in the 2079 Galileo Development compromise and the 2081 Rosiland Amendment; the ITC is founded in December 2085. The Second Martian Revolution section describes colonist representation tensions, the Mars Society for Independence and Freedom, Maisie Luong, the Bradbury Riots, and pro-integration organizing around Lionel Halvidar. It says the ITC was formally absorbed into the federal government in early 2133; by 2134, 50 colonial territories were granted statehood; the 35th Amendment passes the Senate on 30 September 2135; and hundreds of states are brought into the Union on New Year's Eve 2136. The article estimates a 2160 population of 3,003,102,000, identifies nine life zones, describes the Aerophany and other religious groupings, and specifies a 1,004-member Senate and 2,500-member House. It describes the Darian calendar as 24 months and notes a project to move Eris from the Kuiper Belt to help rebuild a natural Martian magnetic field.

## Cross-source continuity and internal consistency leads

1. **Mars commonwealth sequence:** Mars calls itself the second planetary commonwealth admitted; the commonwealth list numbers Earth first, Luna second, and Mars third.
2. **ITC dissolution/absorption:** Mars says the ITC was formally absorbed into the federal government in early 2133. The separately reviewed ITC page (ID 216) gives a formal dissolution date of 1 June 2134 and says its duties were rolled into the U.S. Department of Commerce. The exact institutional transition date/meaning needs reconciliation.
3. **Earth state count:** The Earth page (ID 272) says Earth consists of 145 states, 4 protectorates, and 4 freely associated states; the commonwealth list gives Earth 146 states.
4. **Mars population:** Mars page gives 3,003,102,000; the commonwealth list rounds to 3 billion.
5. **Mars water coverage:** Intro says half the surface is covered in water; later geography says 53% is covered by salt-water oceans.
6. **Mars executive appointments:** Government overview calls the governor and seven constitutional officers "independently elected"; the Executive branch section says the seven other officers are appointed by the governor and confirmed by the legislature.
7. **Mars admission date:** Mars infobox says it was established 4 July 2137; its history describes 2136 statehood/integration actions. The commonwealth table gives 4 July 2137.
8. **Mars state count progression:** Article says 50 colonial territories had received statehood by 2134 and hundreds were brought into the Union in 2136; the 2160 commonwealth table gives 500 states.
9. **Conservative Party chronology:** Infobox founding date is 1 February 2033; narrative describes formation during the 2030s, but the 1 February 2073 meeting is where the "Conservative Union" name is suggested. Treat 2033 as party origin and 2073 as possible rebrand unless another source establishes otherwise; don't collapse these events.
10. **Jacobi/WWIII:** Jacobi's biography places the initial orbital attack on Thanksgiving morning 2051 and reports 7,149 deaths in the initial worldwide wave. The broader WWIII page (ID 107) gives a total war-death figure of 110,000 and different end-date conventions. These may be different scopes (initial attacks vs total war), so preserve the distinction.

## Templates reviewed (125)

The template set consists of 86 `Country data` pages (85 actual data configurations plus the United States documentation page) and 39 other templates. All 85 data configurations have unique aliases; every `flag alias` resolves to a File-namespace title after normalizing underscores to spaces. These are source/configuration reviews, not visual checks of the referenced flag images.

- **Geopolitical infoboxes:** `Template:Infobox country/doc` (88,990 chars) documents the country/territory and geopolitical-organization infobox syntax, extensive parameter families, examples, errors, sandbox, references, microformat and TemplateData. It explicitly notes that geopolitical-organization and micronation infobox names redirect to it. `Template:Infobox U.S. planetary commonwealth` and its 10,960-character documentation page define the adapted state/commonwealth infobox.
- **Commonwealth infobox parameter mismatches:** documentation uses `StateAnthem`, while implementation reads `PlanetaryAnthem`; documentation uses `LieutenantGovernor` / `LieutenantGovernor_alt`, while implementation reads parameters with spaces (`Lieutenant Governor` / `Lieutenant Governor_alt`). The implementation's area/population/elevation labels and links still refer to U.S. states, U.S. state rankings, and a map of the United States, even though the template is used for planetary commonwealth entities.
- **Country data/flag system:** `Template:Flag` delegates to country data and `Template:Flag/core`; 85 data templates define aliases, flag file aliases, alt attributes, and common display parameters. The U.S. documentation page has sections for Earth, Luna, Mars, territories and military flags. The Luna and Mars headings are empty. Its "Earth State flags" list includes many non-U.S. geographic entities (e.g. Alberta, Antilles, Bahamas, Cascadia, England, Guyana, Hispaniola, Ireland, Jamaica, New Zealand, Queensland, Scotland, and Wales), not only U.S. states and D.C.
- **Flag metadata issue:** `Template:Country data European Association` sets `alt attribute = Flag of South Australia`, which does not match its alias. `Template:Country data Oklahoma` contains a leading space in its alias value. `Template:Country data Guyana` uses alt attribute "Guyana" rather than "Flag of Guyana".
- **Date and sorting helpers:** `Template:Start date` and `Template:End date` render date/time values and hidden hCalendar metadata; `MONTHNAME` and `MONTHNUMBER` map month names/numbers; `Dts` invokes `Module:Dts`; `Nts` redirects to `Number table sorting`; `Number table sorting` and `Ntsh` generate hidden sortable numeric keys.
- **Other templates:** reviewed helpers include plain/unbulleted/horizontal lists, date formatting, high-use/intricate/high-risk notices, microformat messages and hCard helpers, person-infobox headers, flag/naval flag helpers, details/hatnote, purge, string-right, citation, icon, and table sorting. `Template:Cite web` checks that URL and title are supplied together and that archive URL/date are both supplied or both omitted.
