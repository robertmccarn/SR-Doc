## Substantive page notes

- **Artificial-Intelligence Operating System (ID 1236):** describes AIOS as a labor-management and automation technology; its adoption is tied to the 2060s–70s automation shock and the Second Depression. The page is relevant to Horace Fadel's labor-policy biography and the Second Depression account. Treat the page's claims as its own narrative, not external technical fact.
- **European Union (ID 1255) and United Nations (ID 1260):** large institutional histories reviewed. The EU page is a separate source from the later European Association; retain the distinction between the historical EU and the postwar/post-2139 political order. The UN page includes a long institutional chronology and should be cross-referenced when reviewing post-Russian-collapse security and later international institutions.
- **Miriam Weinstein (ID 1261), Maya Garcia (ID 1262), Dan Forest (ID 1265), Reed Chen (ID 1266), and the presidential-election articles:** biographical and electoral source set for the 2032 and later presidential sequence. The 2032 election page has a long campaign narrative and several candidates; keep its source-specific candidate sequence separate from later election pages.
- **Second Martian Revolution (ID 1268):** presents a 2125–2131 political movement and armed uprising, distinguishes the broader movement from the 2129 armed rebellion, and notes differing contemporary and later labels. Maisie Luong is named among its revolutionary leaders; the source says the movement continued after the Bradbury Riots.
- **Squirrel Wars (ID 1286):** identifies the Morak First Contact Wars as the human colloquial name; the source's opening describes the IV-103/ISV-03 *Constitution* and a warp-drive/Bubble Trapp failure on arrival at Tu. Preserve the article's own naming variants and chronology for cross-reference with the Moraks/first-contact pages.
- **Union Act of 2073 (ID 1308):** describes the January 10, 2073 act as abolishing Free Associations with Australia, New Zealand, and Britain, annexing them as US territories, and setting procedures for states/territories. It also says Canada was a reluctant signatory and was annexed in 2073, as was Ireland.
- **Android (ID 1311), De-Extinction (ID 1317), and Quantum Economic Model (ID 1337):** technology/society sources. Android distinguishes humanoid robots generally from the post-2050 colloquial use for bio-androids. De-Extinction narrates early cloning milestones and the 2029 influence of Peter Shay's *Humanity's Garden*. QEM describes AI-assisted management/social ownership of primary resources alongside private markets for secondary goods.
- **Lost Decade (China) (ID 1319), Second/Third Chinese Civil Wars (IDs 1413/1408), and Federated Provinces of the Yangtze (ID 1425):** separate phases/entities; don't collapse the 2027–48 Second Chinese Civil War and 2105–26 Third Chinese Civil War into one event.
- **Joseph Loveridge (ID 1343), Maisie Luong (ID 1346), and Second Martian Revolution:** the biographies connect the Guardiola Incident, Martian service, and later conflict. Loveridge's infobox displays a 1979 birth and 2149 death with age 169; that age is consistent only if the later year is reached, but his career spans 2001–2149 with a stated 2059–2085 service gap. Maisie Luong's narrative says she was wounded in the Guardiola Incident but denied an appropriate pension because it was not officially recognized as a war.
- **Party-system pages (IDs 1351, 1362, 1368) and UPP (ID 1523):** the Seventh system begins in 2028; Eighth begins in 2080 after the Second Depression; Ninth begins in 2132 with Lionel Halvidar; the UPP forms in 2153 from the Progress and Union parties plus minor parties. Ninth System describes 2156 as the transition to the Tenth System, while the UPP page says it formed in 2153—these may be distinct party-merger vs party-system transition dates, not automatically a contradiction.
- **2048 presidential election (ID 1370):** internal contradictions are substantial. Infobox says November 3, while prose says Tuesday, November 6, 2048; infobox gives Jacobi 55.2% but zero electoral votes, and Kobe Wilkes 100% / 538 electoral votes, while prose says Jacobi won comfortably. Campaign prose refers to Stanton, Garcia, and Ocasio-Cortez in ways that do not align cleanly with the infobox's named candidates (including a different name for the Republican nominee). Preserve these mismatches rather than choosing a corrected result.
- **Lise Duchesne (ID 1372):** infobox gives New Mexico as office in one field and lists a separate vice-presidency, while prose identifies her as the 54th President, 2073–77. The page contains mostly sparse sections and a typo-like concatenation “becamemore.”
- **Second Vietnam War (ID 1373):** infobox dates February 2, 2102–April 4, 2106; prose says the 2106 Taipei Peace Talks divided Vietnam and declared a ceasefire but that the conflict technically continued until the Third Mexican-American War ended.
- **Horace Fadel (ID 1375), Rayleen Malakar (ID 1377), Stewart M. Li (ID 1378), Estela La Riva (ID 1379), Tom Steele (ID 1380), Seras Stultz (ID 1383), and other biographies:** the reviewed presidential sequence covers the Second Depression, Central American annexation, the Second Mexican-American War, Guardiola Incident, and Second Vietnam War. Several biography pages have empty or skeletal sections; Christopher Reagan (ID 1376) is particularly sparse, with headings for an election and presidency but little/no narrative.
- **Mexico (ID 1391), Edwin Jaso (ID 1444), and President of Mexico (ID 1445):** Mexico's country article runs through the 2139 Treaty of San Juan and annexation. Edwin Jaso's infobox gives presidency 1 Dec 2134–19 Dec 2138; the opening and narrative describe his assassination by his Secretary of Defense and succession by Manuel Portes. President of Mexico is a very large list/history page and opens by saying Portes took office after killing Jaso. Luis Zepeda (ID 1537) has an infobox term 2091–2108, but prose includes a 2096 re-election, a purported “Third Term,” a 2098 constitutional referendum, and a later statement that he won re-election in 2096; this sequence is internally difficult to reconcile.
- **Argentina (ID 1420), Germany (ID 1421), Turkey (ID 1423), Eurasian Coalition (ID 1438), and Trans-Siberia (ID 1432):** national and alliance histories spanning the Cold War, WWIII, and Third Mexican War. Germany's infobox dates dissolution to 10 January 2112; Argentina's infobox has an incomplete September 16 end date. Turkey's page is partly general historical framing before its future-war sections. Eurasian Coalition distinguishes major affiliated combatants (Turkey/Japan) from minor affiliated combatants (Germany/France) and includes additional coalition groupings in its opening.
- **Jupiter (ID 1405), Saturn (ID 1426), Neptune (ID 1427), Orbitals (ID 1477), and Orbital Command Stations (ID 1479):** planetary/commonwealth and orbital-infrastructure articles. Jupiter and Saturn infoboxes both use 4 July 2137 establishment dates and list 2160 populations of 340m and 149m; Neptune gives 122m and identifies its planetary governor through a link labelled for Earth's governor. OCS describes three original stations destroyed on Thanksgiving Day 2051 by Japanese pebble-mob attacks, followed by the crash completion of OCS-104 / *Retribution* and later M-2 Vanguard. Preserve spelling/wording such as “pebble mob” in source notes.
- **Human (ID 1450):** claims 27 recognized human subclades, but its surviving-subclades table has only 10 rows (Naturals, Rainbows, Terrans, Martians, Lunarians, Furries, Avians, Mermaids, Alphas, Dryads). It gives adult population estimates for multiple groups and distinguishes natural humans from modified Terrans.
- **India (ID 1474) and India Crisis (ID 1533):** India page says the United Indian Republic was unified in 2141 from fifteen nation-states after the Third Mexican War. India Crisis infobox dates 2119–2126, while its body dates the triggering cyberattack and cabinet suicides to April 10, 2118 and the US-assisted provisional government to June 30, 2118. The page's later political-disintegration, July coup, and aftermath sections are largely empty. It estimates 30,000 deaths and describes a proxy conflict in the Mexican-American Cold War.
- **Quebec (ID 1551):** says independence referendum passed 64% on November 13, 2027, with formal departure from Canada June 24, 2028; the narrative links the shift in support to Flood damage, famine, and the 2027 market crash.

## Cross-source observations and leads

1. **India Crisis dating:** ID 1533's infobox/body start range is 2119–2126, but the same page's detailed opening chronology begins with the April/June 2118 events. India ID 1474 places final reunification in 2141. This is an internal chronology issue, not yet a resolved canon decision.
2. **2048 election:** ID 1370's election date, candidate identities, percentages, and electoral-vote fields conflict with its own campaign narrative. Reopen the relevant candidate biographies/election pages before any attempted reconstruction.
3. **Human taxonomy:** ID 1450's “27 recognized” assertion is not represented by the ten listed surviving-subclade rows. The source does not explain whether the remaining 17 are omitted, extinct, or otherwise excluded.
4. **Mexico succession:** ID 1444 and ID 1445 both identify Manuel Portes as successor after Jaso's death/assassination; Luis Zepeda's multi-term account on ID 1537 contains a separate unresolved sequence problem.
5. **Political-system dates:** UPP formation in 2153 vs Tenth Party System transition in 2156 are both explicitly stated, and may reflect different definitions (party merger vs system period). Keep as a question, not a definitive contradiction.
6. **First-contact naming:** Squirrel Wars uses IV-103 in the opening and ISV-03 later in the same article for the *Constitution*. Verify against the Moraks article and related mission records.
