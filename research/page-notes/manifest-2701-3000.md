## Main namespace reviews

### Neo-Jeffersonianism (manifest 2702, page 2812)
Political-ideology article tracing Neo-Jeffersonianism to post-WWIII Postnaturalism and the Augment Rights Movement of the late 2060s/2070s. Oscar Lachman is its principal political figure in the 2080s. Positions include meritocratic government, military-veteran cabinet appointments, reduced federal power, stronger cities/localities, immigration restrictions and deportations, trade liberalization, decentralized enterprise, and a more rigid social/family order. The source records opponents characterizing deportations as ethnic cleansing; retain attribution. Cross-check Lachman, Conservative Party, Progressives, Postnaturalism, Augment Rights Act, and 2080 election.

### Through a Dark Mirror (manifest 2772, page 2915)
An explicitly alternate-history/dystopian novel and timeline. Its divergence is tied to the Second Martian Revolution and Mexican victory in the Third Mexican-American War; the imagined later world includes a fractured US, Mexican and Martian empires, Turkish-Israeli dominance in Europe, and a Singapore-led Srivijayan empire. Keep this timeline separate from primary canon unless independently corroborated by non-alternate-history sources.

### Tropical Antarctica (manifest 2794, page 2940)
A speculative-science field guide by Malcolm Moreau imagining a Late Jurassic Antarctic ecosystem around 150 million years ago. It lists fictional dinosaurs, pterosaurs, a flying volaticothere, and a giant filter-feeding ichthyosaur. Cross-reference Moreau's biography and bibliography; these are the book's fictional taxa, not setting-era Antarctic fauna.

### The American Troubles (manifest 2795, page 2941)
A short WIP article describing US domestic terrorism/vigilantism from 2021-2028, beginning with the January 6, 2021 Capitol insurrection. It lists far-right militant groups and rebel police units, federal strength of 1.7 million, and casualties. The infobox calls the result inconclusive and marks the start of the White Tide. Causes and campaign chronology are undeveloped; treat figures as provisional and compare White Tide and US political history.

### Corey Marshall (manifest 2801, page 2947)
Biography of humorist/cartoonist Corey Marshall, pen name Manuel Tiperiter (born 26 October 2019). His cartoons criticize US foreign policy, Evalyn Jacobi-era wartime censorship, imperialism, and Oscar Lachman's deportation policies. The page lists works dated 2047, 2052, 2053, 2056, 2073, 2080, 2086, and 2087, plus three Brickhead Brothers novels (2056, 2062, 2068) adapted into animated films. The earlier unverified claim was reopened and verified. Cross-check Jacobi, WWIII, Second Mexican-American War, Carla Anderson, Horace Fadel, Lachman, and Conservative Party.
