### Vice President of the United States — manifest 3135, page 3246

A 62.7 KB table of US vice presidents, including extensive real-world historical entries and a future extension. It begins with John Adams in 1789, follows the historical succession, and incorporates party affiliations, prior offices, election years, presidents, vacancy intervals, deaths/resignations, and citations. The source is structurally complex (rowspans, nested templates, footnotes, and legacy external citations).

The future extension contains entries through the 2025–2037 period, including Michael Pence (1959–2050), Kamala Harris (1964–2073), and later rows that are malformed/incomplete: dates and birth/death information appear on separate orphaned lines without clearly attached names or offices. In particular, after the 2025–2029 term row, the source has a bare 1964–2059 line with a service range, then election year 2024; following 2029–2037 it has a bare 1989–2093 line with a 2027–2029 service range, followed by election years 2028 and 2032. These should not be assigned to a vice president by guesswork.

Audit priorities:
- The page's legend counts party totals: Pro-Administration 1, Federalist 1, Democratic-Republican 6, Nullifier 1, Democratic 17, Whig 2, Republican 21, National Union 1. Verify these against the full table before relying on them.
- Several historical entries have apparent source/table anomalies: the 1805–1812 George Clinton row is followed by an 1808 election line and a Madison rowspan; vacancy periods are represented as colspan rows; office numbering and rowspans need structured-table validation.
- The page's date/age data contains likely transcription errors and inconsistent date formatting; e.g. the John Adams entry says 1735–1826 and “Lived 90 years” (age calculation depends on exact dates), while many source citations are legacy/dead URLs.
- Future entries need cross-checking against President of the United States, election pages, succession and term-limit rules, and the constitutional amendments. Do not infer missing names from birth/service dates.
- This is a historical office-holder list, not by itself proof of every president's broader political chronology.
