### 181. Hivvir — page ID 2101
Source: `bronze/pages/2101/11066.wikitext`  
A sophont species on Ivrilu in the Kepler-452 system; the article identifies Hiilo as the other intelligent species there. Hivvir are bipedal gliders with patagia. Ivrilu is described as having 63% of Earth's surface gravity and 125% of Earth's atmospheric pressure. Their ancestors are described as six-limbed amphibious cephalopods; later descendants developed a more tetrapod-like body plan with two vestigial limbs. The article gives an approximate height of 1.9 m, independent eye movement, and seven color receptors. Society is organized in tree- or cliff-dwelling family groups using stone tools, wood carving, and cave art; no writing system is reported. First Contact is said to have found them harder to communicate with than the Hiilo, and the page speculates that uplift may be needed for written civilization. The body paragraph spells the related species “Hillo” once, while the rest of the page uses “Hiilo”; preserve as a source typo/variant pending comparison.

### 187. Human — page ID 1450
Source: `bronze/pages/1450/11773.wikitext`  
Defines humans as a clade of sophonts originating on Earth and distinguishes “natural humans” from genetically modified subclades. The text says there are 27 recognized human subclades, while the included table visibly lists ten entries: Naturals, Rainbows, Terrans, Martians, Lunarians, Furries, Avians, Mermaids, Alphas, and Dryads. This is a **coverage discrepancy within the page** (27 claimed, ten tabulated), not proof that the other 17 do not exist. The table gives adult population estimates and distinctive modifications for the listed groups, mostly dated 2155 or 2156. Examples include 700 million Naturals, 7.2 billion Terrans, 2.3 billion Martians, and 500 million Lunarians; the article describes Alphas as brains in vat-grown biorobotic bodies. Mermaids are described as aquatic modifications, and Dryads as arboreal forms. A small number of claims are strongly worded and should be recorded as the fictional source's characterization.

### 191. Hyperion (interstellar vehicle) — page ID 3645
Source: `bronze/pages/3645/11992.wikitext`

The ship table lists Enterprise (IV-101; first flight 2063), Independence, Constitution (destroyed 2067), Challenger, Atlantis, and Discovery. The lead's phrasing about the first ship/first visit is awkward relative to the 2061 launch and the class table's 2063 first flight; record as a chronology/wording lead, not an established contradiction. The image caption itself says “ROUGH DRAFT.” The page's own “Out of Reach” doctrine says ships should drop out of warp near the edge of a star system.

The page leaves “Political disintegration” and “July coup d'état” largely undeveloped. Its final paragraph says later records implicate Mexico's National Intelligence Service.

## Cross-source observations and continuity leads

1. **India Crisis / Joaquín Andrade:** the India Crisis page frames the events as a proxy conflict involving Mexican support and implicates Mexico's National Intelligence Service; the Joaquín Andrade page explicitly credits Andrade with engineering the crisis. These are mutually reinforcing source claims, not an external finding.
2. **India / India Crisis:** the Crisis article places the trigger and opening political events in 2118 and dates the conflict 2119–2126; the India country page separately places later subcontinent unification after the Third Mexican War in 2141. Preserve the interval between partition and unification; do not collapse the two events.
3. **Hong Kong / Japan:** Hong Kong joins the Association of East Asia in 2140; Japan's infobox dates dissolution/succession to 16 September 2141. This may be a staged accession/transition and should be checked against Association of East Asia and China pages before interpreting.
4. **Fadel / ITC:** the ITC article connects the 2079 MarsCorp crash/Second Depression to the Fadel administration's prospects in the 2080 election; Fadel's own page dates the end of his term and defeat to 2081. Check the 2080 election page and presidential chronology before deciding whether this is a discrepancy or merely election-versus-inauguration wording.
5. **Hyperion / First Contact / Hivvir / Hiilo:** Hyperion gives first contact with the K'lerin on 6 April 2065; Hivvir refers to a First Contact context involving the Hiilo and Hivvir. Do not assume the K'lerin contact and the Ivrilu contact are the same event.
6. **Humanity's Garden / Postnaturalism / Refreeze:** the book is said to influence Postnaturalism and the Earth Working Group; Horizon's founding ideology includes Postnaturalism. This is a thematic lead, not proof of party membership or direct influence.
7. **Technology cluster:** Hyperion, Vactrain, Hypersonic Weapons, Japan, Human, and Interplanetary Trade Commission each describe different technology/economic systems. Their dates and dependencies should be reconciled against dedicated source pages, not inferred solely from these entries.
