### 211. Joseph Loveridge — page ID 1343
Source: `bronze/pages/1343/8656.wikitext`.  
Biography of a long-serving US Navy and Space Force officer, born 1979 and died 2149 at age 169. Served in the Navy from 2001–2051, became Vice Chief of Staff of the Space Force in 2051, and helped establish the Lunar Headquarters at Tycho and orbital deployment doctrine during World War III. After mandatory retirement at 80, he settled on Ganymede, later returned to service under the ITC, commanded the Jovian Planetary Guard, and led colonial forces during the Guardiola Incident. He later served as Commandant of the Planetary Guard (2109–2121), then as Space Force Chief of Staff and commander of Planetary Task Force Atlantic during the Third Mexican-American War. The article says he insisted European and African forces remain under his command. He became Chairman of the Joint Chiefs in 2141 and served until death in 2149. The infobox has duplicate `order` fields and labels the first as “United States Space Force 1st” while the office field is Chairman of the Joint Chiefs; preserve as a template/data inconsistency. The narrative calls his later appointment “Space Force Chiefs of Staff” and elsewhere “General of the Space Force”; exact titles warrant comparison with military organization pages.

### 212. Jupiter — page ID 1405
Source: `bronze/pages/1405/9500.wikitext`.  
The Planetary Commonwealth of Jupiter is said to have formed on 4 July 2137, with a 2160 population of 340 million and Galileo as capital. The article says 76% of the Jovian population live beneath Ganymede, Callisto, and Europa; its moon sections give populations of 100m, 180m, and 20m respectively (300m combined), leaving an arithmetic mismatch with both the 340m total and 76% share. Callisto is described as the most populated moon and most tectonically stable; Ganymede has a 5 rem/day surface dose and a 100m population; Europa has a 20m population and subsurface-ocean hive cities. The economy is post-scarcity under the Quantum Economic Model but export-oriented in raw materials and shipbuilding. The article contains no substantive early/20th/22nd-century history, culture, or government sections. The infobox ranks Jupiter fourth in population, while the main text calls it fourth most populous.

### 213. K'lerin — page ID 1138
Source: `bronze/pages/1138/11413.wikitext`.  
The K'lerin (Ornithosapiens cognitus) are the dominant sophont species on Le'ma/Tau Ceti f. They are sexually dimorphic, bipedal, avian-like, with pseudo-feathers and distinctive hands. Their lineage evolved from multi-legged bristleworm-like ancestors; one wetland predator lineage developed larger brains. Most K'lerin remain Neolithic hunter-gatherers; Val're is the principal city-state, historically governed by six matriarchal Queens sharing spouses and dynastic lines. First Contact occurred 6 April 2065, when IV-101 Enterprise landed near Val're. A 2067 uprising followed human introduction of Enlightenment ideas and technology; three Queens died, the remaining three were restored with a representative assembly. The page says the K'lerin Enlightenment produced population growth to 230m by 2160. It also describes three historical civilizations and the T'Vai dynasty. Minor textual variants include Le'ma/Le'Ma and “caravan routs”; these are not silently normalized.

### 214. Kahi'Umknovola — page ID 2040
Source: `bronze/pages/2040/11065.wikitext`.  
The Kahi are bipedal pseudo-tetrapods from Umknovola in the 109 Piscium system, adapted to 27% higher gravity than Earth. Their lineage evolved from sea-cucumber-like ancestors with retractable proboscises and pseudopod-derived limbs. The article claims evidence of written civilization as old as 125,000 years and at least five separate Bronze Age emergences; it suggests earlier civilizations may have belonged to a different species, with the Kahi emerging around 50,000 years ago. Their current civilization is described as roughly analogous to Earth's Roman era, with gearboxes, water/wind-powered mills, textiles, machining, and irrigation but no gunpowder. First Contact is dated 30 June 2110. The article identifies the visiting ship as IV-403 Endurance, but then says “the Discovery's captain” Michelle Hirono led the diplomatic team—an internal ship-name discrepancy. By 2160 the species is said to number 2.8bn, and to have joined the American system as a Free Association in 2158. The article contains an ungrammatical sentence around the formation of a planet-wide government; do not repair it.

### 216. Korea — page ID 1715
Source: `bronze/pages/1715/10807.wikitext`.  
Describes the United Republic of Korea as a unified sovereign state formed from North and South Korea, ending in 2141 and succeeded by the Association of East-Asia. The infobox gives Seoul as capital, Park Nak-yeon as last leader (2021–2141), population 53,011,423 in 2140, and Won as currency. The body says reunification began in 2029 and Korean Unity Day is 11 February 2031; the historical account says the 1945 division and the Korean War in 1950–1953 produced a stalemate, then separately says tensions grew from 2017–2018. The reunification narrative and postwar section describe Japan's invasion during World War III, temporary relocation of the capital to Pyongyang after Seoul fell, US assistance, and later reconstruction. There are many empty historical headings. The article's description of “Korean War in 1950” sits beside a separate set of title pages named Korean War (2017–2019), (2017–2021), and (2018–2021); this needs chronology reconciliation rather than assumed correction.

### 217. Korean reunification — page ID 1651
Source: `bronze/pages/1651/8584.wikitext`.  
A detailed account of North Korea's collapse and reunification with South Korea. It says China ended aid in December 2026 amid the Famine of 2027; North Korea threatened China and South Korea, the Kim regime purged officials, mass defections and unrest followed, and Kim Jong-un was assassinated on 12 February 2028 during a military parade. It describes a 40-country intervention, Pyongyang secured by South Korean forces on 29 March 2029, discovery of labor camps and mines/factories using slave labor, and an estimated 11m North Koreans dead in the 2027–2029 famine. A treaty and phased reunification followed, with Korean Unity Day on 11 February 2031. The article then describes mass migration south, economic integration, organized crime, welfare costs, and later Japan's invasion during World War III. The lead says the North was occupied by South Korea and that the process began in 2029; the detailed account gives a more complicated sequence beginning in 2026. Preserve both.

### 226. Le'ma — page ID 1665
Source: `bronze/pages/1665/11994.wikitext`.  
Le'ma (Tau Ceti f) is described as the only naturally life-supporting object in the Tau Ceti system and the K'lerin homeworld. The article gives a formation age of 5.8bn years, three natural satellites, 12-degree axial tilt, 55% surface water, a single supercontinent for 79m years, and a K'lerisian/Ornizoic geological/ecological framework. It describes an avian-like dominant terrestrial clade with no genetic relationship to Terran birds, live birth rather than egg-laying, large gliding forms, arthropods with larger sizes under higher oxygen, and extensive marine surface ecosystems. The page says K'lerin population is 230m in 2160, matching the K'lerin article. It says the Ornizoic originated “44 million years ago following” (sentence appears incomplete) and refers to the “Greater Puffinwhale,” discovered in 2115. It also notes Le'ma rotates 642 times per orbit, a potentially important worldbuilding detail to compare with its orbital period and planetary climate pages.

### 234. Lionel Halvidar — page ID 212
Source: `bronze/pages/212/11521.wikitext`.  
Long biography of Lionel “Leo” Halvidar (1991–2160), engineer, explorer, Mars terraforming leader, founder/longtime chairman of the ITC, Planetary Guard veteran, and 65th US President (2133–2141). It covers his early Mars migration (2026), Titan/Triton work, Trident Industries, Prometheus/Discovery command, Atlantis terraforming, ITC formation and the Rosiland Amendment, Guardiola Incident, Bradbury commissioner role, ITC chairmanship (2115–2133), Second Martian Revolution/Bradbury Riots, 2132 election, Aztlan secession and Second American Civil War, colonial integration/35th Amendment, Third Mexican-American War and Battle of DC, 2136 reelection, adoption of the Quantum Economic Model, space elevator expansion, nationalization/tax reforms, retirement, family life, and death. It says he was born 3 March 1991 and died on his 169th birthday in 2160. His first artificial body was received in 2049 at age 58 after decades of radiation/microgravity exposure. The page says the ITC chairmanship ran 2115–2133; the infobox gives his ITC term ending 20 January 2133 and presidency beginning the same day. His presidency began amid the Aztlan secession and a civil war; it later integrated colonial territories through planetary commonwealths and federal institutions. It describes a Mexican nuclear-flare/orbital assault on Washington on 26 August (year context 2135) and a 29-hour battle ending with colonial fleet relief at 4:21 a.m. on 27 August. He won 81% in the 2136 election. He later retired to Mars and died on 3 March 2160. The article itself contains a major sequence issue: it first says the Camp Navajo attack began the civil war and describes Halvidar's 15 April mobilization, then later dates the Camp Navajo attack to 2 February 2133. The two dates should be checked against the Second American Civil War article. Other notable details: his infobox party history says Union Party 2131–Present despite his death in 2160; “Rosiland” appears here, matching one spelling in the ITC article while that article elsewhere uses “Rosalind”; the page uses “Halivdar” and “Halivdar's” in a few places. The full article is long; its section structure is retained in the source and should be revisited when cross-referencing the civil war.

## Cross-source observations and continuity leads

1. **Korea chronology:** Korea's country page says the Korean War was in 1950–1953, while three captured page titles form a redirect chain labelled 2017–2019 → 2017–2021 → 2018–2021 → Korean Crisis. The reunification page separately describes a North Korean collapse beginning 2026, Kim's assassination in 2028, intervention in 2029, and Unity Day in 2031. These may be distinct conflicts, or title/date errors; inspect Korean Crisis if it exists elsewhere and compare the First Korean War/modern conflict pages.
2. **Kahi first-contact ship:** Kahi'Umknovola names IV-403 Endurance as the away-team ship but calls the captain of Discovery Michelle Hirono. Compare the Endurance and Discovery source pages.
3. **Jupiter population arithmetic:** 20m Europa + 180m Callisto + 100m Ganymede = 300m, while infobox total is 340m and text says 76% reside on those moons. The figures do not reconcile as written.
4. **Le'ma/K'lerin:** the K'lerin and Le'ma pages agree on a 230m population in 2160 and connect the species to first contact on 6 April 2065. Keep separate from Hivvir, whose source places them on Ivrilu.
5. **Halvidar chronology:** his biography's Camp Navajo timeline puts the 15 April mobilization before a later explicit 2 February 2133 date for the triggering attack. Compare the Second American Civil War and United States of Aztlan pages.
6. **Halvidar / ITC / MarsCorp:** the biography repeats the ITC's 2081 Rosiland Amendment and QEM integration story, while the earlier ITC page used both “Rosiland” and “Rosalind.” Check amendment source and preserve spelling variants.
7. **Liberal Party founding:** infobox date 2052 conflicts with body date 2029. The article also links its 2070 coalition/Horizon formation to the post-World War III political landscape; compare Horizon and party-system articles.
8. **Latin American Union:** the UPG is described as an alliance including Mexico during the Mexican-American Cold War. Its political relationship to Mexico's leadership and the later US victory/annexations is underexplained because its history sections are empty.
9. **Latvia / Poland / European Association:** Latvia's sequence runs from integration/annexation by Poland to a 2076 Federation of Poland and the Baltic, then to EA provincial status after the Third Mexican-American War. Compare Poland, EA, and war articles for exact transfer dates.
10. **2160 population table:** the brief 2160 list gives 9bn total Earth population and 1.33bn India; compare the detailed Population growth table and all country pages before interpreting demographic trends.
