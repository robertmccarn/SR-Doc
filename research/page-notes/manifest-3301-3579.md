## Talk and Thread namespaces: editorial history

Key leads:
1. **Point of divergence and COVID-19:** 2020 discussion explicitly asks whether the creator would revise the timeline in response to the ongoing COVID-19 crisis or preserve a defined alternate-history divergence. 7. **Creator permission:** a user asks to add a character; creator response requests a short bio and says additions must be relevant to the timeline.
8. **COVID and timeline revisions:** discussion of whether the timeline should remain a possible future demonstrates a recurring editorial issue: present-day events and assumptions were being revised in real time.
